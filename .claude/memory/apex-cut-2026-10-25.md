**Premise held.** `corner_fold` ON leaves MARINE's M, A and N byte-identical
and refuses the acute tips (`_FOLD_WHY`: `long_way`, `off_ring` -- the
outline arc round an acute tip is longer than 1.6 x the legs' widths).

**His rule** (`tools/letter_pieces.py` crosses, one reader on both files,
apex reader in the PR): through every tip whose legs are 35-75 deg apart he
sews one piece, crosses within 16 deg of the line of text, spanning both
strokes 1.0-2.4 W (7 mm at most), down to the point; above that cut each
stroke is its own column. Legs 90 apart (MFab's square A: two L corners) he
FOLDS -- corner_fold's case. Contradicting rows: MARINE's A top and N
top-left are its sheared SLAB (his slab piece, crosses 50-88 deg); MARINE's
N foot keeps stem and diagonal side by side and merges only at the
baseline; his merge on MFab's W left V starts lower than the crotch (where
the merged cross is ~1.7 W).

**Built:** at each lone crotch, the nearest convex corner along its bisector
that the strokes CLOSE on is a tip -- halfway the cross <= 0.7 of the cut's
and four-fifths <= 0.52 (V-like), or holding width to halfway then <= 0.36
(a shoulder: the M's V between its stems at 101.9 mm); the closing rate
puts the legs under 80 deg apart; the tip piece reaches the point, holds
no junction (concave corner) past half a stroke from the cut, except on
another cut or at the point, and its two own edges nearest the cut's ends
meet at 20-80 deg (MARINE's A at 101.9 mm: the right leg past the crossbar,
aimed at its foot's corner, closes like a tip but its sides are parallel;
runs under min(0.5 W, 1 mm) are crotch chamfers and are skipped). The axis is re-aimed through the middle of
the cross 60% of the way on (a flat top's corner and a chamfered crotch
both skew it, 22 deg on the W). Walk from the crotch to the first cross
<= min(2.4 W, 7 mm); cut it (in two at the crotch when it passes through
it: a line through a ring vertex is not noded), join the crotch to it, drop
the rules' cut from that crotch. The tip piece is stationed square to the
apex axis (`straight_axis` reads a wide-short tip as a slab). When that cut
lands 40%+ of the way to the tip (a V hanging between stems: MARINE's M at
76.5-95.7 mm, whose crotch cross runs on through the right stem), retry AT
the crotch with the other concave corners' edges continued as bounds and
cuts (`_apex_stems`), kept only for a shoulder-then-point V: the 76.5 mm M
then pivots no column at all. OFF md5 equal on all seven jobs and on the
four captured letters.

**Traps:** judge each tip against the junction rules' cuts only -- the W's
three crotches share one height and one tip's cross ends on the next
crotch. A tip test that splits with only the apex cut leaks the tip piece
round the whole letter when the cross ends on another cut. The pro_pair
crop is one window on both frames: the pro's MARINE sits 2.5 mm higher in
his extents. `columns._tuck_stations` walks a leg across the new tip piece
to tuck under a stem 5.6 mm away (`_span_graph`'s merge radius 1.3 W + 0.7
on MARINE): 1.2-1.5 mm of leg on the tip, OFF too -- columns.py's, not cut.

A speculative "drop a crotch half that leaves its leg a scrap" rule refused
the 101.9 mm M outright (both leg tops under 0.4 W^2): removed.
