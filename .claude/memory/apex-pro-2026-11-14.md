# The pro's V, built as he sews it — 2026-11-14

Kent on apex_cut + guard (Becker hat large M): "Off was closer to the pros
here, but still not perfect" / "I want it exactly like the pros." Both stay
OFF.

**Read stitch by stitch** (`tools/apex_pro.py`, `letter_pieces` crosses,
pro registered per letter). All five Becker files are one design scaled, so
the M's V is the same in each:
- The RIGHT leg owns the point. Level crosses start AT the point and step up
  the V and on up that leg.
- The left leg stops on a split from the crotch down-left, continuing the
  right leg's inner edge. It is one fan (104 -> 137 deg) that also covers
  the left stem's top.
- The stem ends level at the crotch's height.

The N's two tips also go to the right-hand stroke. A's apexes are caps
(ProSeal level, Becker a flat upright band), not a leg.

**Instrument:** angle error (deg per mm2 of shared thread, last stitch
wins) + split-line offset (mm). It said apex_cut ON (10.4 deg) was already
closer than OFF (16.6) on hat large, against Kent's eye. The ON floor's level
crosses match his level point, but ON's seam (1.53 mm) is where his is not.

**Built OFF:**
- `outline_cut._apex_pro_cuts`: scope is a down V only.
- `_apex_pro_split`: the split.
- `_apex_pro_shoulder`: the stem's level cut.
- `_apex_pro_stem_cut`: an ext on the owning side.
- `_apex_pro_column`: the level column.

**Traps:**
- `find_cuts` clusters a chamfered notch into one corner, so the stem cut
  reads `corners()` itself (at 95.7 mm the V went bare without it).
- Level scanlines take the LONGEST segment: a V merged with a stem loses
  the V.
- `cfg.extra` keys are refused by the service's `_parse_config`. Set them
  after parsing (the runner patched `_parse_config`).

Not built: the walk's order through the joint (the pro buries the travel
from the fan to the point under the level crosses), and the N's tips.
