# Arc re-set: measured, not shipped — 2026-11-14

The Director's next-wave brief (Kent approved it 2026-11-14) said: "the
small-text re-set only grows horizontal lines, so Bridge's arc text is never
raised (the pro raises small text 1.3-2.2x)". **Don't build it again without
a design whose arc text is actually small.**

**What was built (PR first commit, then removed).** `small_text_arc_reset`
worked like this:
- A cluster the horizontal pass refused, whose letter centres fit a circle,
  is UNROLLED: polar about the centre, arc length as x, radial as y, feet
  down. The shapes and the source raster go with it.
- The same pass re-sets it there. Only the moved letters are rolled back.
- The cap is read off the retraced letters.

On a synthetic 1.7 mm word on a 12 mm circle it raised the line x2, on its
circle, in order. It is kept in the PR's history (the PR's first commit,
`refs/pull/<n>/head`).

**Why it was not shipped.** It changed nothing in Kent's three kits: the
design md5 was the same OFF and ON on every arc-text design.
- **Bridge hat (62.9 mm):** the arc letters are 3.875 mm radial, retraced
  (the polygons read 3.7-4.2). With the cap at 4.0, `tall_enough` is right.
  REPORT.md's "art 2.69" missed the dark letter outline.
- **The pro's x1.2 is not a raise.** Read on 12 px/mm renders the same way,
  ours is 4.0-4.1 and his 4.7-4.9. He got there by REDRAWING THE EMBLEM:
  the wave and inner arc are gone, the anchor is bigger, and the band sits
  1.5 mm further out.
- **No room in our band:** it leaves about x1.1 before the 0.8 mm
  clearance, at any size.
- **Grunge TOWER MACHINE:** 4.84 mm, `tall_enough`.
- **Lineart EYE FOR blind child:** 8.4 mm, and its letters fit no one
  circle.

**Respine: measured, NOT shipped.** `small_text_reset_respine` re-read a
grown letter's dark-ink bean spines from its grown polygon, for Fremont's
EAT | STAY | PLAY (the re-set grows it; stage 7 sews spines written at the
art's size).
- By eye it read WORSE: the skeleton of the fattened letters invents
  letters. EAT became "BAJ", STAY "S7A(", PLAY "PLA)".
- OFF's art-size beans read better (`reset-scatter-1113/respine/`).
- It is in the same first commit.

**Shipped OFF (`cfg.extra`).** `small_text_reset_report`: the re-set's rows
reach the metric `small_text_reset` (via `Generation` / `PipelineResult`).
They say why a line was skipped (`skipped`, `blocked_by`). On Fremont they
name both EST 1895 halves: `no_room`. The 1.89 mm cluster is blocked by a
thread-2 shape spanning the whole design (92 x 50 mm); the 1.85 mm one has
an empty `blocked_by`.

**Bridge's real faults** are merged, blobbed letters at 4.4 px/mm, and the
green U carved into a bean (`flat_dark_carve_word_guard`, not built). The
grunge render loses its black field, its M and most of MACHINE, OFF and ON
alike.
