# Arc re-set, respine, re-set report — 2026-11-14

The Director's next-wave brief (Kent approved it 2026-11-14): the small-text
re-set only grows horizontal lines, so Bridge's arc text "is never raised".

**Built OFF (`cfg.extra`).**
- `small_text_reset_report`: `enlarge_small_text`'s rows reach the job's
  metrics as `small_text_reset` (via `Generation` / `PipelineResult`). The
  rows say which lines grew and why the others were skipped. Metrics only.
- `small_text_arc_reset` (`small_text._arc_line`): a cluster the horizontal
  pass refused (`tall_enough` / `not_one_line`) whose letter centres fit a
  circle is UNROLLED (polar about the centre: arc length x, radial y, feet
  down), shapes AND raster. The same pass re-sets it there; only the moved
  letters are rolled back. Its cap is read off the RETRACED letters.
- `small_text_reset_respine`: a grown letter carrying dark-ink bean spines
  gets them re-read from the grown polygon (Fremont's EAT | STAY | PLAY).

**What the eye found (sheets in `eye_pairs_out/reset-scatter-1113/arc/`).**
- **Bridge's arc letters are not small.** They are 3.875 mm radial
  (retraced; the polygons read 3.7-4.2). REPORT.md's "art 2.69" under-read
  the dark letter outline. The re-set cap is 4.0, so `tall_enough` is
  correct.
- **The pro did not raise these letters; he redrew the emblem.** He dropped
  the wave and the inner arc, enlarged the anchor and moved the band out
  1.5 mm. Read on 12 px/mm renders the same way: ours 4.0-4.1, his 4.7-4.9
  (x1.2).
- **The band leaves no room** to raise ours at any size: about x1.1 before
  the 0.8 mm clearance.
- **Kit result:** grunge TOWER MACHINE (4.84 mm), lineart EYE FOR blind
  child (8.4 mm, not one circle) and Bridge all come out ON = OFF, md5 the
  same.
- **What the synthetic arc shows:** a 1.7 mm RESTAURANT on a 12 mm circle is
  raised x2, on its circle, in order, with no two letters touching.

**Bridge's real faults** are merged and blobbed letters at 4.4 px/mm, and the
green U carved into a bean (`flat_dark_carve_word_guard`, not built). The
grunge render loses its black field, the M and most of MACHINE, OFF and ON
alike.
