`fill_angle_pro_slant` (cfg.extra, OFF, PR #1350) slants each fill +/-20 deg.
Its write-up measured direction kappa against the pro rising from 0.311 to
0.395. Kent chose "Eye sitting first" (11-08), and the sitting
`fill-slant-1108` ran on the 22 pro jobs: 16 live pairs, 6 byte-identical.

Kent's picks (11-09): ON 0, OFF 7, tie 9.
- The pre-registered mirror rule reads favours OFF: (ON - base)/decided = -1.00,
  with a design bootstrap of [-1, -1].
- The decided pairs span only 4 designs, and Becker gives 4 of the 7.
- Controls: 0 of 4 repeats were answered differently; 3 of 4 identical pairs
  were called a tie.
- Record: `docs/eye-pairs-2026-11-08/fill-slant/SITTING.md`.

**Kent's ruling (2026-11-09):** "Keep OFF, record (Recommended)". The flag and
its code stay in the tree, unused.

**Why:** closer to the pro's fill direction (higher kappa) did not look better
to Kent. He never preferred ON, and mostly could not tell the two apart.

**How to apply:**
- Do not re-propose the flip on the kappa result alone.
- A new attempt needs a different slant rule and a new sitting.
- Matching the pro's direction is not, on its own, a reason Kent's eye accepts.
