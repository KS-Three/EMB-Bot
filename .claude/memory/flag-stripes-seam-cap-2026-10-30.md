[[pull-comp-by-stroke-2026-10-22]].

cap at a grazing angle), then `place`'s body-less ladder stepped the rail in 15%.

end rails on the edge. Corner notch 0.4 mm gone.

counted the pull twice, 0.85 mm past art on the third red, and its union left a 55 mm
slit that forked the skeleton and split that one stripe. The other six lost theirs to a
floating-point gap, so tongues on rail satin were already mostly absent.

show only the top thread; Simulate is where it appears.
