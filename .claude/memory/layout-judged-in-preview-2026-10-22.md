2026-10-22, when asked for a sew-out date so eight OFF flags could flip, Kent:
"You don't need a fucking sew out for stitch layout, you have a previewer that
does that already, pull weight and comp I can see, but stitch layout and photo
conversion quality we can preview ourselves."

**Why:** gate 1 ("no sew-out, no physical constants") had been read as "every
pro-gap flag ships OFF until fabric". That stacked eight unflipped flags in one
evening (fill slant, cross underlay, cut every jump, pro preset values,
pull-by-stroke, short-column underlay, tie_in_off, compact_solids_satin) and
the engine stopped improving on his machine. Kent sees that as struggling.

**How to apply:**
- A change in WHERE stitches go (satin vs fill vs ring, cut vs float, turn
  shape, tie placement, underlay presence, column construction, which shapes
  get borders, photo conversion) is judged from OFF/ON renders. Build it,
  render before | after | pro, and put the flip to Kent as a preview decision,
  or ship ON when the renders prove it and the moved pins are shown.
- A change in HOW MUCH thread fabric pulls (pull comp, satin density/spacing,
  fill row spacing, stitch length as density) still needs a sew-out. Build OFF.
- Fine lettering is the standing priority ("I continuously state fine lettering
  needs to be improved"; "start to finish plug and play digitizer"). Work
  towards it; plumbing is overhead, say so when it eats the evening.
- He asked why it feels like struggling: name the real blocker (flags parked
  OFF, lettering is construction not flag values, plumbing), not activity.
