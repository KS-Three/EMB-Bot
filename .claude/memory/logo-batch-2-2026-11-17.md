Kent, 2026-11-17: "do ten new logos next". Ten public-domain US government
logos from Wikimedia Commons, 960 px PNG, Studio request, left chest, all at
90 mm, main `796123f2`: AmeriCorps, Department of Energy seal, Fish and
Wildlife Service seal, HHS, FBI seal, GSA, NIH/NCI, National Weather
Service, SBA, CDC. None of the art or renders is in the repo. Commons
rate-limits `Special:FilePath` (429): fetch one every 20 s.

**Read by eye beside the art.** Good: AmeriCorps, GSA, National Weather
Service, NCI. Fine at this size: Energy, FBI (its ribbon words are gone).

**CDC: the tagline was cropped off. FIXED, ON (Kent 11-17: "Ship as a fix,
ON", shown the before | after).** The PNG's page is TRANSPARENT and the
tagline stands on a transparent strip under the blue panel. RGB under alpha
0 is black, so `letterbox.detect_letterbox` read 147 uniform black rows as a
screenshot bar and `strip_letterbox` cropped 726 -> 579 rows before stage 1.
Now `_bar_holds_art`: a detected bar that is mostly transparent by its alpha
yet holds opaque pixels is the page under artwork and nothing is stripped.
An EMPTY transparent bar still goes (`test_alpha_is_cropped_with_the_image`
models one). Of 66 images (testdata and both Commons batches) the detector
fires on 3 and only CDC is transparent, so only CDC's framing moves:
23,493 -> 29,150 stitches, trims 77 -> 93. `tests/test_letterbox.py` (+3).

**Catches on CDC after the fix (not fixed).** With the page seen, 13 white
rays read as enclosed background and are left unsewn (the 11-11
enclosed-white rule; `SHAPES_LEFT_UNSEWN`). The tagline's first line sews
thin and readable, its second ("AND PREVENTION") fat: two tiers in one
line. The gradient panel is a patchwork either way (gradient class, 6
threads).

**SBA subtitle (Kent's pick): traced; `small_print_floor` repaired, still
OFF.** "U.S. Small Business Administration" at 90 mm: lowercase 2.6 mm tall
on 0.7 mm strokes, all satin in the Column lane. Why it fuses: stage 4
drops every hole under 0.56 mm2 (the e's eye, the B's upper counter are not
in the traced outline at all) and the 1.1 mm counters that survive close
under the thread's width. Two routes that do NOT apply: the bean line
(`bean_letter_max_stroke_mm`, 0.5 in the Studio's true-size set) is read
nowhere while `bean_only_lines` is ON (Kent's "a letter is never a bean"),
and `dark_ink_bean_by_width` needs a sewn ground. `lettering_word_tiers`
alone moves nothing.

`small_print_floor` (built OFF 2026-10-12, its two constants gate-1
candidates for the hairline card) is the existing cure: it restores dropped
counters and holds thread off them. ON, it had a fault of its own: its
branch in `columns.column_runs` took EVERY column of a floored letter and
has none of the lane's later floors (`thin_floor`, `_STROKE_FLOOR`,
`_RING_FLOOR`), so a piece under the cross floor was dropped. SBA lost each
t's crossbar, an m's first arch and an r's arm: letters with no counter.
Kent 11-17, shown that picture: "Fix the m and t under it". Fixed: only a
column whose zigzag comes within the clearance of a counter takes the
branch (`_zigzag_near_counter`, read on the rails BEFORE `_clear_counters`
moves them; read after, every ring sits exactly the clearance off and
reads as not near). Flag OFF is untouched. ON after the fix, SBA: m, t and
r whole, a / o / d / B counters open, the e keeps its eye; 11,373 (OFF) ->
11,415 stitches, trims 19 both. `tests/test_small_print_floors.py`; the
small-print, cored-counter and floor files pass (81).
