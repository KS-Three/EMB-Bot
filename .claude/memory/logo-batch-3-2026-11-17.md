Kent, 2026-11-17: "do ten more logos". Third batch, Wikimedia Commons,
960 px PNG, Studio request, left chest, 90 mm, main `5b57bc19`: Interior
seal, Coast Guard seal, Education seal, Labor seal, Veterans Affairs seal,
FCC, FTC seal, OSHA, Office of Personnel Management seal, USDA. Art and
renders are not in the repo.

**Read by eye beside the art.** Good: FCC, OSHA, USDA (shape), Labor,
Education.

**Coast Guard: the ring text was buried.** "UNITED STATES COAST GUARD
1790": 25 navy strokes, dark-ink beans (thread 3332), sewn in block 3; the
white ring (0015) sewed in block 4, over them. `thin_ink._ground_of` names
a stroke's ground as the shape whose OUTLINE (holes ignored) covers most of
it. Outlines nest: the aqua field's and the white ring's both cover a ring
letter whole, the comparison is strict, so the first in list order won (the
field). `ink_after_ground` then saw a ground in an earlier layer and moved
nothing.

**Built OFF: `cfg.extra["dark_ink_ground_innermost"]`** (`_ground_of(...,
innermost=True)`, passed to `bean_dark_ink` and `ink_after_ground`;
`tests/test_dark_ink_ground_innermost.py`): among outlines that cover the
stroke equally, the smallest. Coast Guard ON: ground = the white ring, the
letters sew right after it and read; 44,503 -> 44,514 stitches, trims 131
-> 132. Catch seen: the white ring renders greyer ON (thread still 0015;
cause not confirmed, likely the ground's fill sewn through under the
letters). The 46-design OFF/ON comparison was started and not finished when
this was written: see the PR body for its result.
