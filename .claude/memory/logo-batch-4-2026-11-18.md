Kent, 2026-11-18: "do ten more logos". Fourth batch, Wikimedia Commons,
960 px PNG, Studio request, left chest, 90 mm, main `8051f118`: Marine
Corps emblem, NASA meatball, NIH, and the seals of Agriculture, Commerce,
Homeland Security, Justice, State, Transportation, Treasury. Art and
renders are not in the repo.

**Worst fault: Commerce's ring text.** Bold white "DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE /
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA" on a blue ring rendered as hairlines. Traced:
stage 5 plans it right (44 holes kept, letters 0.96 visible). White is the
first thread, so the letters (satin, 1.0-1.3 mm strokes) sew first and the
ring fills round them. Each fill row is laid 0.3 mm long at both ends for
pull, so the blue covers about 0.6 mm of each stroke. Not a bridge and not
travel: the rows stop at the holes, just long.
`dark_ink_refuse_ground_gaps` does not touch it.

**Built OFF: `cfg.extra["thin_letters_after_ground"]`**
(`thin_ink.thin_letters_after_ground`, called in `plan_stitches` after
`ink_after_ground`; `tests/test_thin_letters_after_ground.py`). The
lettering of one thread on one ground, sewn before that ground, moves to a
layer right after it when the line's MEDIAN measured stroke is under
`THIN_LETTER_PULLS` (6) x the pull (1.8 mm at 0.3). Stage 5 then runs the
ground under the letters. Two things learned building it:
- A per-letter width test split the line into fat and hairline letters
  (strokes read 0.97-1.30 around a 1.2 limit). Judge the line whole; the
  thread's untagged narrow shapes on the same ground (stars, a letter the
  text read missed) move with it.
- A ground must be a FILL (not itself a narrow column). Without that the
  Agriculture seal got worse: cream highlights inside its yellow letters
  and beads jumped on top, trims 284 -> 416, and To a T Machine (customer
  logo) moved. With it both are byte-identical.

**Measured, 56 designs** (batches 1-4 and the 16 repo logos at 80 mm):
Commerce 39,010 -> 39,221 stitches, trims 114 -> 100, one more thread
block (white is revisited); AmeriCorps 31,849 -> 31,347, trims 36 -> 21,
one more block. Both read bold like the art. The other 54 are unmoved (the
function returns its input). Not run: the `scratch_kent` paired corpus; the
6 x pull limit is a layout candidate, not a sewn number.

**Seen, not fixed:** State's glory sews as a teal blob and its shield
stripes brown; Agriculture 218 s, 284 trims, palette off (a shaded seal);
Marine Corps globe folds to tan (colour cap six) and reads "UNITFD";
Justice loses its stripes and motto; Homeland's eagle colour is off. NASA,
NIH, Transportation and Treasury read acceptably.
