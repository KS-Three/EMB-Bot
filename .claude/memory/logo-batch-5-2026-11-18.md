**Classifier look (Kent 11-18: "Look at the classifier"). Measured, nothing
changed.** 66 designs under the shipped gate (`tonal_frac` >= 0.11): 12 read
`gradient`, 1 `photo_scene` (MFab hat), 53 `flat`. Ten of the 12 are really
shaded (Transportation 0.29, Coast Guard 0.47, Park Service 0.50, Interior
0.31, OPM 0.24, CDC 0.18, Secret Service 0.17, Marine Corps 0.16, Defense
and Veterans Affairs 0.13, FBI 0.12). Two are flat art:
- **BLM, 0.120.** The counted pixels are only the anti-aliased EDGES between
  colours of close lightness (mountain / hills / river): local sd under
  `TEXTURE_STD_HI`, so they are not "hard" and land in the band or ramp.
  A flat logo with low-contrast neighbours reads tonal. Changing that is a
  signal change = gate 2.
- **HUD seal, 0.647.** The file's white disc is alpha 253 (77% of the
  foreground is 127 < alpha < 255, 98% of that exactly 253).
  `alpha_edge.extend_opaque_colour` treats every pixel under 255 as
  non-opaque and gives it its nearest fully opaque pixel's colour, so the
  whole disc becomes a blue/green smear and stage 0 counts the smear. A bug
  in what is measured, not a threshold. HUD still sews well in the gradient
  lane. Only one other file of the 66 has much semi-opaque foreground:
  `becker_marine_logo.png` (27%, alpha spread 211-254), the file that
  routine was tuned on (2026-09-20), so a change there needs Kent and
  Becker's pictures.
Near the gate on the flat side: Justice 0.113 (flat by the confidence
floor), Agriculture 0.101, Labor 0.088.
