**Read well:** NIST, HUD, FAA, Smithsonian, Social Security.

**Peace Corps R (traced, fixed behind a flag).** The R of CORPS (9 mm, 2.1
mm strokes) is cut once, at its leg. Stem and bowl stay ONE folded column
whose 45 stations run round the bowl and top serif; the stem, the largest
part of the letter, takes no stitch. `lettering_columns_cover_handback`
cures it, but Kent ruled that flag 11-14 ("Keep OFF, record"; don't flip,
don't re-narrow) and named the real fix: sew the dropped branch in the lane
([[monarch-size-cliff-2026-11-13]]).
**Built OFF: `cfg.extra["lettering_columns_sew_dropped_branch"]`**
(`columns.dropped_branches`, read in `lettering_columns_shape`;
`tests/test_dropped_branch.py`). The parts of a column's piece its stations
do not reach (at least W x W, surviving an erosion of W/4) are cut as
letters of their own and sewn with the rest; kept only on `absorb_gains`.
66 designs (batches 1-5, 16 repo logos at 80 mm): 9 change, 57 identical.

Security's M regains its stem; Veterans Affairs' F whole; CDC's blue field

worse. Doubt: a fine stitch shows on a T arm of the Justice seal. Not run:

ruling named (neither is in this corpus).
