**Half bold, half thin lettering: traced to `lettering_columns_off_line_mark`
(ON since 11-18, another lane's flip, [[off-line-mark-2026-11-18]]).** That
rule reads "stacked on a mate, no row-mate" on axis-aligned boxes, which is
also every letter down the SIDE of a ring. It tags them
`lettering_off_line`, sets tier `fill`, and they lose their bean spines.
DEA at 90 mm: 11 of 50 ring letters (all dark-ink, one group, median width
0.636 under the 0.75 bean limit) sewed as fill beside bean line-mates. Its
own corpus showed "1 of 35 moves"; on these 74 designs it marks letters on
22, more than one per cluster on 20.
**Built OFF: `cfg.extra["off_line_mark_single_only"]`**
(`columns.off_line_marks(single_only=)`, tests added to
`tests/test_off_line_mark.py`). A cluster with MORE THAN ONE off-line
member marks none. 74 designs (batches 1-6, 16 repo logos), guard ON vs
main `ea4257d0`: 20 change, 54 identical; MFab hat and LC keep their one
mark each (the rule's own case), as do EPA, Interior, Treasury. DEA 33,788
-> 33,640 stitches, trims 36 both; Supreme Court 36,538 -> 36,452; Marine
Corps returns to 43,079, its count before the off-line flip. Looked at
closely: DEA and Supreme Court (clear gain). The other 18 change a few
letters each and were judged from whole-design sheets only. Still marked
after the guard: one letter each on Coast Guard, Veterans Affairs, OPM,
SEC, two on Education (a lone off-line letter in its cluster): a ring can
still lose one letter this way.
