Senate seal, Space Force seal, Supreme Court seal, CIA seal, DEA seal, NRC
logo, SEC seal. Two of the ten downloads were the SAME art as earlier
batches under another file name (`US-DeptOfTheTreasury-Seal`,

new. Art and renders are not in the repo.
