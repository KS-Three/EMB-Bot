Kent, 2026-11-19 ("#1 and #2": the Marshals dashes, then ten more logos).
Eighth batch, Wikimedia Commons, 960 px PNG, Studio request, left chest, 90
mm, on the branch of #1687 (`lettering_columns_sew_dropped_hairline` ON,
`thin_letters_after_ground` ON, the ring-text guard ON): Court of Appeals
for the Armed Forces, Court of Federal Claims, Veterans Affairs seal (a
second drawing of it), GAO, Library of Congress, Merchant Marine, NSA,
Public Health Service, Tax Court. `Seal of the United States Coast
Guard.svg` is the SAME art as batch 3's `US-CoastGuard-Seal` (identical
stitch count), so nine are new. Art and renders are not in the repo.

**Seen on the overview, nothing traced or built:**
- Merchant Marine: the whole ring line ("UNITED STATES MERCHANT MARINE",
  black on white) is lost; 149 trims. The worst fault of the batch.
- Mixed weights in one line of ring text, again: NSA ("NATIONAL" sews
  hollow, "SECURITY" and "AGENCY" bold, "UNITED STATES OF AMERICA" thin);
  GAO ("BILITY" and "OFFICE" bold beside thin letters). The ring-text guard
  is ON here, so this is another cause than the off-line mark.
- Court of Federal Claims: thin serif ring text garbled ("FBDBXAL"), the
  motto too.
- Library of Congress: ring stars garbled; 108 s for 11,091 stitches.
- Court of Appeals: laurel wreath sews heavy black; ring text thin but
  reads.
- Veterans Affairs (this drawing): flag stars and stripes break up.
- Slow: Veterans Affairs 189 s, Public Health Service 143 s, GAO 134 s.
- Read well: Tax Court, Public Health Service.

Commons file names that exist, pattern `Seal of the United States X.svg`:
Coast Guard, Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces, Court of Federal
Claims, Department of Veterans Affairs, Government Accountability Office,
Library of Congress, Merchant Marine, National Security Agency, Public
Health Service, Tax Court.

**Merchant Marine ring text (Kent's pick, 11-19): traced, a flag built
OFF.** The black keyline round the eagle and the disc is ONE ring;
`silhouette_underfill` reads black as the colour covering most of its
outline, makes the ring its own under-fill, and merges every black shape
inside -- the 0.5-0.7 mm ring letters too, each in a hole of the white disc.
The disc sews on top, rows laid long for pull, and the holes close: the line
is gone. `thin_letters_after_ground` never saw them (merged before it runs).
**Built OFF: `cfg.extra["underfill_keeps_thin_letters"]`**
(`silhouette_underfill._keep_thin` / `_hole_ground`; `plan(keep_thin=)`;
also `thin_ink.ink_after_ground(other_thread=)`: the dark-ink letters had
named the black under-fill as their own ground and still sewed under the
white; tests in `tests/test_underfill_keeps_thin_letters.py`, 12). A thin
shape (`THIN_LETTER_PULLS` x pull) in a fill's holes stays out of the
under-fill only when that ground's holes hold a kept LETTER of its thread.
No new constant. First cut (every thin shape) moved 24 of 91 designs, most
on star points and badge shading; narrowed: 10 move, 81 identical.
Merchant Marine 35,449 -> 36,761 stitches, trims 149 -> 139, the whole
line reads. Marshals: blue field to navy, strays gone (+3,000 stitches,
trims 118 -> 131). OPM: star points and eagle head/tail sew white (the text
reader tags OPM's E and W star points as text, so they count as a line).
CIA INTELLIGENCE fuller; National Guard a few strays fewer; Justice, NSA,
FBI, Defense about the same. Worse: Agriculture, one cream patch left bare
beside the grey band (the ground-of-own-thread half). Batch-8 sweep ran
while main was merged in, so movers were re-run both ways on one tree.
