Kent, 2026-11-19 ("1&3": NSA/GAO, then ten more logos). Ninth batch,
Wikimedia Commons, 960 px PNG, Studio request, left chest, 90 mm, with
`underfill_keeps_thin_letters` and `dark_ink_line_one_lane` ON (#1687's
branch): the state seals of Alaska, Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Nevada,
Ohio, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Wyoming. All ten exist on Commons as
`Seal of <State>.svg`; Hawaii, New Mexico, Montana, Idaho and Kansas were
on the list and not fetched (ten reached). Art and renders are not in the
repo.

**Seen on the overview, nothing traced or built:**
- Nevada: ring text (dark serif on gold) garbled and half lost; the scene
  folds toward white and grey. The worst text fault of the batch.
- Wyoming: the lettering on the yellow sashes (EQUAL RIGHTS, LIVESTOCK,
  GRAIN, MINES, OIL) is lost and the sashes sew as fat yellow blobs; the
  figures' faces sew yellow.
- Texas: white ring text on light blue sews faint (low contrast in the art
  too); the star loses its facets.
- Arizona: shield colours fold (green hill and the miner sew black).
- Florida: the scene breaks up; 329 trims, 292 s.
- Slow: Colorado 477 s (382 regions), Florida 292 s, Utah 169 s, Oregon 164 s.
- Read well: Colorado, Ohio, Utah, Oregon; Alaska's ring text.

**Nevada ring text (Kent's pick, 11-19): traced, a flag built OFF.** The
ring letters were never text-tagged (the only "line" the reader found was
the 36 sunburst teeth), so `dark_ink_line_one_lane` cannot reach them. The
bold serif letters and the solid stars share one dark-ink group on the gold
ring; `bean_dark_ink(by_width=True)` reads its median MEAN width (2A/P)
0.51 mm < 0.75 and every stroke sews a one-line bean: handwriting, not the
art's bold serif. Serifs and star points are long outline round little
ink. Their CORE (2 x `shapely.maximum_inscribed_circle` radius) is 1.18.
Census, 143 groups over 101 designs: NO gap in either measure (core
0.27-2.36 continuous); NOAA 0.66, FEMA 0.57.
**Built OFF: `cfg.extra["dark_ink_bean_by_core"]`**
(`thin_ink.bean_dark_ink(by_core=)`, `_core_width_mm`,
`DARK_INK_BEAN_MAX_CORE_MM` = 2 x `machine.SATIN_MIN_CROSS_MM` = 1.0, a
layout threshold judged from renders; tests in
`tests/test_dark_ink_bean_by_core.py`). 101 designs: 36 move, 29 visibly.
Gains: Nevada (trims 117 -> 98), SEC ring text bold (159 -> 119), Court of
Federal Claims, Florida, Agriculture ring text, Supreme Court ribbon.
Worse: Hotel Fremont patch EAT | STAY | PLAY to blobs (rope better), House
ribbon dark grey, Energy Star field top darker, Fish & Wildlife ring text
heavier and rougher, OPM eye a blob. Mixed: Ohio's sheaves solid (trims
91 -> 101), CDC trims 70 -> 78, Defense wreath sews blue.
The 11-19 sweep hit a full disk (26 GB of old sweep renders in the
scratchpad): delete old `out/` dirs before a long sweep.
