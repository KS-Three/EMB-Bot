Kent, 2026-11-19 ("1&3": NSA/GAO, then ten more logos). Ninth batch,
Wikimedia Commons, 960 px PNG, Studio request, left chest, 90 mm, with
`underfill_keeps_thin_letters` and `dark_ink_line_one_lane` ON (#1687's
branch): the state seals of Alaska, Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Nevada,
Ohio, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Wyoming. All ten exist on Commons as
`Seal of <State>.svg`; Hawaii, New Mexico, Montana, Idaho and Kansas were
on the list and not fetched (ten reached). Art and renders are not in the
repo.

**Seen on the overview, nothing traced or built:**
- Nevada: ring text (dark serif on gold) garbled and half lost; the scene
  folds toward white and grey. The worst text fault of the batch.
- Wyoming: the lettering on the yellow sashes (EQUAL RIGHTS, LIVESTOCK,
  GRAIN, MINES, OIL) is lost and the sashes sew as fat yellow blobs; the
  figures' faces sew yellow.
- Texas: white ring text on light blue sews faint (low contrast in the art
  too); the star loses its facets.
- Arizona: shield colours fold (green hill and the miner sew black).
- Florida: the scene breaks up; 329 trims, 292 s.
- Slow: Colorado 477 s (382 regions), Florida 292 s, Utah 169 s, Oregon 164 s.
- Read well: Colorado, Ohio, Utah, Oregon; Alaska's ring text.
