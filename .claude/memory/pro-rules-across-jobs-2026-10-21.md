2026-10-21, Kent asked to read the pro's rules across ALL his files before building
more Fremont-derived behaviour. Full write-up: `docs/pro-digitizer-rules-2026-10-21.md`
(renders in `docs/renders/pro-rules-2026-10-21/`).

Headline: the Fremont lettering habit is general. Text under ~4 mm cap is raised to
3.65-4.25 mm on every job that has such text (Fremont, Drone, Proseal), condensed
(width grows 0.64-0.90 of the height growth), bold satin (columns 0.9-1.26 mm), gaps
closed to 0.15-0.35 mm. Text above ~4.5 mm stays at art size on 6 jobs. The pro's
column width is NOT art + fixed pull: thin strokes go to a floor, wide ones stay at
the art's width (+/-0.4). New and clean: every beanie gets a 0.5 mm-row knockdown
fill under the whole design, sewn first (5 of 5 beanie jobs). Five jobs sew white /
hollow interiors (badge fields, hollow letters). Every dense fill has a ~1.0 mm cross
underlay. Satin spacing 0.40 on every hat/LC file.

**Why:** these are what Kent can encode as engine behaviour (craft) vs a customer
option (design changes: sew enclosed white, flatten gradients, rope removal).

**How to apply:** count trims from the DST, never the PES — pystitch reports a TRIM at
every PES jump (Fremont patch: 53 as PES, 20 as DST). Proseal's art is only in
`scratch_kent/` — measure it there, never commit it. Floors (0.9 mm column, 0.5 mm
knockdown rows) are gate-1 candidates until sewn.
