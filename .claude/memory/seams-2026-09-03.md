# Seams — the instrument, the card block, and what the underlap rule actually leaves — 2026-09-03

- **"No overlap at the seams" is false in code and partly true on cloth.**
  Stage 5 extends the earlier colour `pull + overlap_mm` under the later
  one (0.55 mm on pique). `tools/seam_underlap.py` reads what a design
  actually carries: the synthetic logos get the full 0.525; Hotel Fremont
  gets 0.237 mm mean over 1,549 mm of seams, 673 mm under 0.25.
- **The shortfall is the hole floor.** A hole that growing the shell would
  push under `min_detail_mm²` is held at its original size, and the seam
  around it gets no tongue. Small counters and thin later shapes on real
  logos are exactly those holes. Any fix is a design decision (grow the
  tongue without closing the hole, or accept), not a constant.
- **Card block 6** decides `overlap_mm` on cloth: pairs at 0 / 0.25 / 0.5 /
  1.0, rows across the seam. Card is 66 x 96 mm now.
- **Blend bands underlap by `overlap_mm`** earlier-under-later (darker by
  chart L*); `_BAND_OVERLAP_T` is gone.
- Instrument caveat: the seam length includes the corner where the earlier
  colour's own pull growth wraps the later's edge (a 10 mm butt joint reads
  ~9.7), so quote depths and rung fractions, not absolute lengths, when
  precision matters.
