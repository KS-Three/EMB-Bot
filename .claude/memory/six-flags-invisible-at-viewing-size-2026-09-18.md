On 2026-09-18 Kent judged 34 labelled before/after pairs (shipped vs one flag ON) on the nine
`REAL_ART` logos: `satin_polygon_axis="artwork"`, `satin_rails_follow_edge`, `design_angle`,
`satin_per_stroke`, `satin_patch_junctions="satin"` and `classify_area_weighted`. He called
29 "no difference", 3 "both bad" (enthusiast/polygon_axis, drone and screenshot/area_weighted),
and 1 each way (rails better on tires, design_angle worse on tires). His ruling: record it and
stop. All six stay OFF. Full table: `docs/kent-review-2026-09-18.md`.

**Why:** the renders do differ. With stitch texture blurred out, up to 38% of a design changed
(bridge/polygon_axis), but the change is close-range stitch quality (cleaner satin columns),
not how the logo reads.

**How to apply:** do not propose flipping any of these six as the fix for "it doesn't look
as good as Ember" ([[kent-eye-vs-instruments]]); whatever holds that gap is visible at
arm's length, and these are not. A flip can still be argued on close-range stitch quality,
thread or trims, but not on the look. The judging pace was about 10 s a pair, an at-a-glance
read, and the page gave no pointer to where each pair changed. The three "both bad" logos
are the open lead. Related: [[eye-pairs-build-2026-09-17]].
