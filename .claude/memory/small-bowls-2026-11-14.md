Small bowls, 2026-11-14 (#1558). Bukit's "beyondcoal" lost its small bowls
in `column_runs`' cross floor. A small_bold ring column's thin top and bottom
crosses (0.33-0.49 mm after inset + pull) dropped while 12 side crosses
stayed; `thin_floor` only widens under 3.

`cfg.extra["lettering_columns_ring_floor"]` (built OFF) widens a bowl wall's
thin crosses instead: the o goes 0.70 -> 1.00 cover and the counter stays
open, at 85.1 and 80.4 mm. Layout, not a physical constant (gate 1: floor and
pull untouched).

**How to apply:** on a closed ring a dropped cross is a gap; never drop
crosses on a column with no ends.

**Kent 2026-11-15:** ticked ON. Ruled ON 11-15, flip pending.
