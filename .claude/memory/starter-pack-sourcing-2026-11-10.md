**Ruling (Kent, 2026-11-10, three AskUserQuestion picks):** the launch starter pack is **12 designs**. Each is **strict CC0 or Public Domain Mark vector art, digitized by EMB-Bot's own pipeline**, topped up with designs drawn in the Studio. There is no commissioning, no commercial library, no CC-BY (it would need a credits screen), no SA/NC/ND/"personal use", and no logos or brand marks, because CC0 does not clear trademarks. Provenance is logged per design: author, source URL, licence as shown on the page. Openclipart uploads flagged `pd_issue` are excluded.

**Why:** the research (`docs/starter-pack-sourcing-2026-11-10.md`, PR #1381) found no clean supply of openly licensed STITCH files. The emoji sets are ShareAlike or NC, and the "free" sites ban redistribution. The rule is the same one the fonts were held to: pull anything uncertain. Commissioning cost $300-1,440, and pricing pages never said who owns the file.

**How to apply:** any starter-pack work checks every art file against this allowlist and records its provenance in a manifest. A 24-design pack, or adding CC-BY art, is a new question for Kent, not a default. Related: [[free-digitizer-survey-2026-11-04]].
