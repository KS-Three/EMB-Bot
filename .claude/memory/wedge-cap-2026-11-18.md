Prime grader 11-17: the h's stem "built from offset segments with visible steps" (`toat_machine_hat`).

**Cause (measured):** script words go through `script_strokes.script_columns` (pen-stroke skeleton, crosses cast to
the ink), then `columns.column_runs`. The stem is ONE `curved` column of 61 stations; where the c's tail and the
arch's hairline merge into it the ART is wider, so its cross runs 3.1-3.4 against 2.3-2.5 with a step at each end.
`stage6_satin.satin_stroke` is NOT the stem's sewer: it only runs in stage 7's lane-OFF ordering pass, so every
`_rail_points` change (gap hold, envelope, ramp, bulge cap) was inert on the plan (final stem byte-identical).

**Fix built OFF (`cfg.extra["lettering_columns_stem_wedge_cap"]`, `wedge_cap.py`):** a column gives back a wedge
(opened width profile, a step of 0.3 mm at both ends) only to a THINNER sibling (median cross under 0.85 of its own)
whose piece already covers the ground; siblings read as already capped. Evidence in
`docs/renders/wedge-cap-2026-11-18/`: 2 of 35 move (hat's h better but a low seam remains; Bridge no visible
change), bare artwork 0 -> 0, `satin_cover` 0.9954 -> 0.9955 on the h.

**Trap:** the first rule (any covering sibling) let the thin c-tail column give its ground across the stem to the
stem, which then had nothing to hand over: the stem kept its wedge and 5 of 35 moved with nothing to see.
