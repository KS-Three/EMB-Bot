# Bean census: what the reference digitizer sews as a triple run, and what we do (2026-10-27)

Generated by `python -m tools.bean_census --corpus` (arm `off`, 17 jobs, 141 s wall). Tables only; the rule and its reading are in the PR that added this tool. Regions are OUR segmentation's; the reference file is read in its own frame through `pairframe.register_pair`.

**The rule, in words.** Over 17 paired jobs the reference digitizer sews **17 retraced (bean) runs, 780 mm in all**: 16 of them TWO passes and 1 three passes, at a median pitch of 1.31 mm, on 8 of 1101 regions. By the region of ours they sit on: BODY 6+ to colour 5, BODY 4-6 to colour 3, LETTER 0.6-1.2 3, DETAIL line on body 2, LINE between 1, LINE inside 1, BODY 2-4 to colour 1, LETTER 1.2+ 1. What they follow is a LINE the art draws as a stroke of another colour than its surround, too thin to satin: 8 of the 11 runs on or in a filled body ride such a stroke (the drone's panel and edge lines, a line-drawn gun grip), and the rest are a TM mark, a 30 mm line-drawing stub and short stubs on letters; the drawn stroke under the 6 runs inside a body reads 0.69-6.02 mm (median 3.61). He beans the EDGE of a filled body on 4 of 231 bodies, every one along a drawn outline stroke (ours 1: the edge cap and the run tier ring bare edges), a small solid under 2 mm on 0 of 81 (ours 1: his is one satin blob, or dropped), and small lettering under 1.2 mm on 1 of 251 letters (ours 111: he raises small text to satin). Thin regions of our segmentation (LINE rows): 2 of 457 (ours 24).

**Where ours disagrees most, by count:** small LETTERS sewn as bean letters (111 regions against the reference's 1), then thin LINE regions the run tier or the satin fallback rings (24 against 2), then bodies (1 against 4: the run tier's ring on a sub-floor solid and the bean edge cap). The one place the reference beans and ours does not is a drawn detail line INSIDE a body our segmentation has no region for (2 of his runs).
