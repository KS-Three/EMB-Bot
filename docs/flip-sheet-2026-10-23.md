## What each number is

- `fill_turn_slant`: stitches under 0.5 mm. Sets `{"fill_turn_slant": true}`. **PR #1036 was RED on CI when rendered (tests/test_cut_every_jump.py test_off_is_byte_identical_to_base[earlier-0], an OFF md5 pin stale after main moved); rendered from its branch, diffed against that branch's own OFF.**
- `fill_underlay_cross`: underlay read under each fill (verdict: fills). Sets `{"fill_underlay_cross": true}`.
- `cut_every_jump`: uncut jumps. Sets `{"cut_every_jump": true}`. **PR #1035 was RED on CI when rendered (the same OFF md5 pin as #1036); rendered from its branch, diffed against that branch's own OFF.**
- `sew_enclosed_white`: stitches in white thread. Sets `{"sew_enclosed_white": true}`.
- `trim_bury_under_later`: trims whose hop later thread covers. Sets `{"trim_bury_under_later": true}`.
- `compact_solids_satin`: edge-cap rings (plan roles). Sets `{"compact_solids_satin": true}`.
- `small_solids_satin`: edge-cap rings (plan roles). Sets `{"small_solids_satin": true}`.
- `tie_in_off`: run starts that lock. Sets `{"tie_in_off": true}`.
- `satin_underlay_short_columns`: satin columns under 5 mm sewn bare. Sets `{"satin_underlay_short_columns": true}`.
- `small_text_set`: median small-letter height, mm. Sets `{"small_text_cap_mm": 4.0, "small_text_cap_max_width_scale": 1.05, "small_text_cap_min_stroke_mm": 0.85, "small_text_cap_letter_gap_mm": 0.25, "small_text_cap_bold_scale": 1.45, "lettering_columns_small_bold": true}`.

Readers: stitches and trims are pystitch records (the pro read as DST, never PES: the PES reader reports a TRIM at every jump). `stitches under 0.5 mm` are needle-to-needle. `fills read BARE` is `tools/pro_fill_underlay.py`. `uncut jumps` are needle-up hops with no TRIM or colour change round them. `white thread` reads the file's own colours (the pro's PES; a DST has none). `hops under later thread` is `tools/trim_classes.py`. `edge-cap rings` are plan run roles, so the pro has none. `run starts that lock`: the first three stitches after a cut, colour change or jump all at most 1.0 mm with a reversal. `short columns bare` is `tools/pro_satin_underlay.py` (PR #1044's branch). `letter height` is the median of `tools/small_text_columns.py`'s 0.8-6 mm pieces.
