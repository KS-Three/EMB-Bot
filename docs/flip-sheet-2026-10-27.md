## What each number is

- `monoline_script_satin`: stitches under 1 mm (whole design). Sets `{"monoline_script_satin": true}`. **The catch is TRIMS (the st / tr figures): PR #1063 whole design 8,001 st / 10 tr OFF -> 6,101 / 24 ON, pro 5,555 / 10. In the word: under-1 mm stitches 2,515 -> 548 (pro 43), fill -> satin square to the stroke 0.00 -> 0.88 (pro 0.85). PR #1063 was open when rendered: run from a local merge with main (not committed).**
- `silhouette_underfill_rim`: closed satin loops (borders). Sets `{"silhouette_underfill": true, "sew_enclosed_white": true, "enclosed_white_keyline": true, "silhouette_underfill_rim": true}`. **Acts only under silhouette_underfill, so OFF is arm d2 (under-fill + white sewn + keyline) and ON adds the rim: the ring host's outer edge as one closed satin loop at 1.75 mm after its fill, in its colour. The pro: MFab one closed column 1.94 (hat) / 1.86 (LC), Fremont the white ground's edge in two passes at 1.39-1.73. Becker's letter host gets none (its ring is the satin).**
- `fine_bars_satin`: shapes sewn as a plain run ring (plan roles). Sets `{"fine_bars_satin": true}`. **Free-standing thin ink bars under the area floor: To a T's speed rules, Fremont's separators, the drone's AND DRONE rule, MFab's LLC hairline. The pro satins each; ours rings them OFF. Gate-1 caveat (config.py): no width, pull or density moves, but whether a ~1 mm bar holds on a cap is the sew-out question small_solids_satin already carries -- Kent flipped that one from renders on 10-23 with the same caveat.**
- `lettering_columns_apex_cut`: stitches under 1 mm (whole design). Sets `{"lettering_columns_apex_cut": true}`. **An acute tip -- the M's V, a W's apexes, a pointed A or N -- sews as ONE column cut off its strokes along the cross, as the pro's does (#1088; #1090 skips the slot rule for a letter with such a tip: MARINE's M at 95.7 mm). Corner fold is ON on main.**
- `text_cluster_part_touching`: shapes the text tagger put in a line (the Column lane's input). Sets `{"text_cluster_part_touching": true}`. **Two letters the tagger misses on MFab's AND WELDING: the hat's I and N touching at a 0.08 mm neck (traced as one region), and the LC's AND N overshooting the cap height. Alone, the LC's newly tagged N goes bean on its white keyline and sews hollow; ON + BEAN INK adds `bean_ink_outlined_letters` (#1128), which sews it satin. The hat's plan does not move under bean ink (#1128), so its third cell is left out.**
- `outline_loop_mitre`: closed satin loops (borders). Sets `{"outline_loop_mitre": true}`. **The border_from_outline loops (ON on main) keep their far rail at the satin pitch through a letter's convex corners: the pro's corner is a round pivot, not a mitre (far ends 1.00 W out; ours OFF 1.1 mm apart at the corner, his 0.4). Bridge's art outline is guarded out (#1078), so Bridge should read no change.**
- `keyline_trio`: white stitches (under 1 mm). Sets `{"keyline_fold_slivers": true, "skip_covered_shapes": true, "extra": {"absorb_fragments": true}}`. **Not a flag: the detection pass. keyline_fold_slivers, skip_covered_shapes and absorb_fragments all ON at once on every non-MFab job that sews white (since #1112; MFab has all three through the gate, #1122). Where its DST equals OFF none of the three acts on that job; where it differs, each flag gets its own cell.**
- `lettering_columns_italic_lean`: stitches under 1 mm (whole design). Sets `{"lettering_columns_italic_lean": true}`. **Under lettering_columns_text_lean (ON on main): the bars of an italic word cross along its stems, 71-78 deg off the line of text as the pro's do on PROSEAL, where the lane crosses them square to themselves.**
- `fill_section_travel`: travel runs in the plan (pro: n/a). Sets `{"extra": {"fill_section_travel": true}}`. **A cfg.extra key (the Studio's request cannot set it; the cell lays it on). Fill sections join by a route hidden inside the field instead of a cut. PR #1091 read Fremont's in-piece cuts 51 -> 41 on its own base and its other hats unmoved; on this sheet it changes six of these seven jobs (Gaulke byte-identical), but the DST's trims by one at most (Becker large +1, MFab hat and the drone -1).**
- `bean_ink_outlined_letters`: shapes sewn as a plain run (bean letters). Sets `{"bean_ink_outlined_letters": true}`. **MFab's small line is black letters on a black body, each ringed by a white keyline, so the ink reader takes the keyline for the letter's ink (0.4-1.7% of the spine inside the letter). OFF, the LC's AND A goes bean and sews as a white letter with a thin black line round it. ON leaves out a read whose spines lie less than half inside the letter: the A sews black satin (crosses 44 -> 116, pro 114). The hat is the control: its reads are the letters' own ink and fail the shape gate, so it should not move.**

Readers: stitches and trims are pystitch records (the pro read as DST, never PES: the PES reader reports a TRIM at every jump). `stitches under 0.5 mm` are needle-to-needle. `fills read BARE` is `tools/pro_fill_underlay.py`. `uncut jumps` are needle-up hops with no TRIM or colour change round them. `white thread` reads the file's own colours (the pro's PES; a DST has none). `hops under later thread` is `tools/trim_classes.py`. `edge-cap rings` are plan run roles, so the pro has none. `run starts that lock`: the first three stitches after a cut, colour change or jump all at most 1.0 mm with a reversal. `short columns bare` is `tools/pro_satin_underlay.py` (PR #1044's branch). `letter height` is the median of `tools/small_text_columns.py`'s 0.8-6 mm pieces.
