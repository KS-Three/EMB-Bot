Open `renders/flip-sheet-seam-order-2026-10-30/index.html` for the pictures side by side. The pro's crops are not committed (Kent 2026-10-27: "Stop new ones, keep old (Recommended)"): they are written to `pro_renders/`, gitignored, and `report --inline` puts them on the local page. ## What each number is

- `seam_order_read`: cuts inside one piece / all cuts. Sets `{"photo_prep_background_removal_timeout_s": 900}`. **The flip read backwards: OFF pins fill_seam_order False, ON is the shipped engine (ON since #1198). Becker beanie and the drone are the rows that went the wrong way; MFab LC is the gain to keep.**
- `knockdown_shorter_walk`: cuts inside one piece / all cuts. Sets `{"photo_prep_background_removal_timeout_s": 900, "knockdown_shorter_walk": true}`. **The beanie knockdown sewn with and without seam order, the shorter walk kept. ProSeal beanie is the control: its walk is shorter WITH seam order, so a blanket skip would have cost it 147 mm of exposed travel.**
- `fill_seam_order_priced`: cuts inside one piece / all cuts. Sets `{"photo_prep_background_removal_timeout_s": 900, "fill_seam_order_priced": true}`. **Seam order's two progressions: fewer ridges and flips first, _score settling a tie, instead of the shorter flown chord.**

Readers: stitches and trims are pystitch records (the pro read as DST, never PES: the PES reader reports a TRIM at every jump). `stitches under 0.5 mm` are needle-to-needle. `fills read BARE` is `tools/pro_fill_underlay.py`. `uncut jumps` are needle-up hops with no TRIM or colour change round them. `white thread` reads the file's own colours (the pro's PES; a DST has none). `hops under later thread` is `tools/trim_classes.py`. `edge-cap rings` are plan run roles, so the pro has none. `run starts that lock`: the first three stitches after a cut, colour change or jump all at most 1.0 mm with a reversal. `short columns bare` is `tools/pro_satin_underlay.py` (PR #1044's branch). `letter height` is the median of `tools/small_text_columns.py`'s 0.8-6 mm pieces.
