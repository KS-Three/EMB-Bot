## #1 priority, by a wide margin: schedule a physical sew-out session

**Nothing has ever been sewn on a real machine, anywhere in this project.**
Every quality claim in `MASTER_SCOPE.md` — for every capability area — is
geometry checked in software, not verified thread-on-fabric. This is the
single biggest confidence ceiling across the whole app, and it's the one
action that unblocks the most other open items at once:

- Settles the **DST codec axis bug** (browser DST is transposed vs.
  Tajima/pyembroidery standard — confirmed, unresolved, Kent's call whether
  to fix it, since every existing EMB-Bot DST file would be affected).
- Verifies **fabric-preset accuracy** (README literally asks users to "stitch
  a test on your machine and tell me if a fabric needs tuning" — nobody has
  yet).
- Resolves **fill row spacing (law 19)** — a real two-population disagreement
  (0.20mm vs. shipped 0.40mm) that only a physical stitch-out can settle.
- Confirms **PES/EXP export correctness** on a real Brother machine or in
  PE-Design, not just pyembroidery cross-validation.
- `docs/hardening-closeout-2026-08-02.md` already specifies **four hoopings**
  that would resolve **nine** currently-open geometric questions in one
  session.

This is explicitly flagged everywhere as Kent's call to schedule, not
something to push on — but it's worth stating plainly that it's the highest-
leverage single action available right now.
