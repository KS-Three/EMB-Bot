## What the flag does

`digitizer_core/column_junction.py`, called from `stage7_sequence` after `overlap_run`. A stitch of a later satin run of the
shape that has a partner in an earlier satin run (same station within 0.3 mm, cross direction within 30 deg, extents that overlap
by 0.2 mm or more and are 0.3 mm or more offset, union at most 4.4 mm) is stretched across the union of the two. A stretch of 8 or
more such stitches counts. The earlier bar's partner stitches become a 2 mm running path (the `column_overlap` construction), so
the needle path stays unbroken. No satin floor, pull or cover figure is read or changed.

**Pile, found and fixed.** First build (ramp 4, every partner counted): at F4b the share of the covered area 3 or more deep went
0.51 to 0.71. Cause: B stitches were widened over A stitches that were NOT removed (a partner in a lap shorter than 3 consecutive
stitches stays satin), so the end of the block sewed two layers where there had been one. Fix, in the flag's own code: a partner
counts only where the earlier bar's stitches can come out (a lap of 3 or more consecutive ones), and each end ramps in over 16
stitches. Measured on the captured stage-7 runs (0.4 mm lines, 20 px/mm, satin only):

| window | OFF | first build | fixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| F4 (x 41.5-46.5, y 9-14): 3+ deep, max layers | 0.43, 13 | 0.57, 13 | 0.44, 13 |
| F4b (x 44.5-49.5, y 10-15) | 0.51, 10 | 0.71, 8 | 0.51, 9 |
| F4 whole (x 41-52, y 8.5-15) | 0.42, 13 | 0.59, 13 | 0.42, 13 |

Covered area in the F4 window goes 15.4 to 14.7 mm2 (the ramp narrows the block at its ends, where the earlier bar's stitches stay).

## Why faults 3, 5 and 8 are the next fix, and are not this

They do not change OFF to ON. In each, the two satin pieces meet at an ANGLE, so no partner passes the 30 deg cross-direction test:
- **3 (35, 6)**: two satin runs meet at a corner and fan; the cross directions differ by a corner's angle.
- **5 (36, 17)**: one satin stitch of the F stem bridges a 1.65 mm gap where the dash's bars join (a gap, not an overlap).
- **8 (88.5, 18.5)**: the slot's bar meets the leg's fan wedge, again a corner.

They are corner fans and wedges. The lead for the next fix is the pro's corner: his F arm is groups of 5-6 stitches 2.5-3.5 mm long
at about 20 deg that turn the corner in one piece, where ours fans two runs into a point. A corner builder belongs beside this one in
`stage7_sequence`, on the same earlier / later run pair, with an angle test in place of the parallel test.
Fault 1 (the M notch) is the black mark and the white under it; the white there is a corner too.
