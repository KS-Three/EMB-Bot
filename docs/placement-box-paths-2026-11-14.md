# Placement boxes: which path clamps to which (2026-11-14)

Why this exists: PR #1493/#1495 recorded an open mismatch. The box
`buildImportedDesign` clamps a stitch file to was not the box auto-fit and the
Studio's typed/drag clamps use. This maps every path per garment x placement,
measures the gap, and recomputes the #1495 overflow table.

**Kent's ruling, 2026-11-14: "Hat front only."** The hat front's clamp box is its
PLACEMENTS box, 113.5 x 64.0 on every path. The left chest and the beanie stay
split on purpose; the test `test/placement-box-paths.test.js` pins both, with the
ruling in a comment, so nobody "fixes" them. The numbers below are generated by
`tools/placement-box-paths.mjs` (`node tools/placement-box-paths.mjs`), which
probes the engine rather than copying a table.

## Answer

- **Before the ruling the paths disagreed on 3 of 28 placements**, all the
  *default* placement of a garment the pro measured: `hat_front` (import clamp
  127.0 x 57.15, everything else 113.5 x 64.0), `left_chest` (101.6 x 101.6 vs
  96.0 x 76.5, area cap 5,800 mm2) and `beanie` (114.3 x 63.5 vs 94.5 x 56.0). The
  other 25 placements agree to 0.1 mm on every path. **Now they disagree on 2**:
  `left_chest` and `beanie`. `resolvePlacement` rewrites `widthIn/heightIn` from
  the PLACEMENTS box for any non-default placement, so the inch pair *is* the
  PLACEMENTS box there.
- **The inch pair is not a hoop field, and it was not a deliberate second box.**
  `GARMENTS[].widthIn/heightIn` (`src/garments.js:13-24`) is the original
  round-inch *placement* table. The physical hoops are `HOOPS`, a ceiling check,
  never a clamp (`app/src/lib/hoop.js`). When the pro boxes arrived
  (`PRO_PLACEMENT_MM`, PR #1110/#1118, 2026-10-26) and `PLACEMENTS` after them
  (PR #1159, 2026-10-27), the Studio's clamps moved to the new box
  (`SizePanel.svelte:69`, `EmbroideryField.svelte:499`); the engine's builders
  did not (the memory note for #1159 lists it under *Not done*).
- **What the ruling fixes.** A digitized upload on the hat front is auto-fitted
  into 113.5 x 64.0, the service sews it at that size, and the result re-enters
  `buildImportedDesign` (`generate.js:129`), which used to meet the 57.15 mm
  height clamp. A square logo fitted at 64.0 mm was drawn at 57 mm, 10.7% smaller
  than the fit said, with no note. In the running Studio (red-disc.png, a
  square, on Hat Front): canvas **57x57 mm before, 64x64 mm after**
  (`docs/hat-front-one-box-2026-11-14/hat-front-before.png`, `...-after.png`).
- **How it is built.** `src/garments.js` `CLAMP_ON_PLACEMENT_BOX = ["hat_front"]`
  puts `clampBox` (the hat's `PRO_PLACEMENT_MM` entry, by reference) on that one
  garment; `clampBoxMm(garment)` returns it, else the inch pair. The five clamp
  readers below use it. `resolvePlacement` clears it on the hat's non-default
  placements, whose own rewritten inch pair already is their box. No box value
  changed; `{ proSized: false }` still gives the round-inch box.
- **Still open (not decided): left chest and beanie.** Recomputed under the
  PLACEMENTS box for those two as well, overflows would go 76 -> 78: 4 beanie
  combinations worse, 2 new beanie overflows, and 10 more fit but are drawn at a
  different scale (5 starters shrink 3 to 18% on the default left chest, which is
  where the starter pack lands). The beanie width (94.5) is the least firm pro
  number (his two wide beanies disagree, 93.8 vs 110.5), and the left chest's
  area cap cannot be expressed in a clamp. See the last tables.

## Generated tables

Rows are measured by probe: a design thousands of mm wide (then tall) through
`buildImportedDesign`; a 5,000 mm typed width through `buildQualityDesign`;
`fitScale` on the same extremes; and `placementBoxMm`. The probe's engine output
rounds to 0.1 mm stitch units. The first overflow section is the ruling itself
(before vs after, hat front); the second is the part still open.

**Paths still disagree on 2 of 28 placements**: left_chest, beanie (Kent's ruling 2026-11-14: hat front only; left chest and beanie stay split on purpose).
