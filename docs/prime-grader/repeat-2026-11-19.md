# Prime grader repeat test, 2026-11-19: the grader against itself

**Question.** How far does the nightly verdict move when nothing in the engine
changed? Every scorecard says the grader's repeat agreement "is not measured
yet". This measures it.

**Setup.** One capture and one kit on main `2c6bb99`, nine designs, rubric
2026-11-18 (`9a169b106243`), flat-ended renders. The same kit was graded three
times, each time by nine fresh blind readers (27 in all), each given RUNBOOK
A.3's brief word for word except for the verdict folder, all on the worker
model the nightly uses. Readers did not see each other's verdicts: each
round's folder was moved out of `digitizer/` before the next round started.
Nobody looked at the pictures before all 27 verdicts existed. Scored with
`python -m tools.prime_grader repeat` (RUNBOOK C). Every figure here is an
instrument reading, not a quality claim (ROADMAP gate 4). One kit, nine
designs, three rounds: small n, and the intervals show it.

## What this means for reading a nightly card

1. **A one-step grade move on one design, on one night, is noise.** Two
   readings of the identical render differ by one step on about one design in
   seven, so on a nine-design card expect roughly one such move a night with
   nothing changed. A one-step move is worth a look only if it repeats the
   next night or several designs move the same way.
2. **A two-step move was never seen without a change.** It stays the PR-body
   alarm RUNBOOK A.6 already makes it.
3. **The grade scale is effectively two-valued here** (Okay or Bad). The
   kappa interval reaches below zero because of that, not because readers
   scatter widely: the gap they do disagree on is the Okay/Bad line.
4. **A single reader's fault is more likely a one-reader fault than a
   reproducible one.** 62% of the places one reader marks gross are marked by
   no second reader in the same class within 3 mm. The faults that do
   reproduce are far above chance (0.39 above, against 0.03 chance), so the
   reader is finding real things, but which instances it pins varies.
5. **Some classes are reliable, some are not.** Letter shape, stray/blob
   artefacts and gaps reproduce at 0.5 to 0.9. Edge and pen flow reproduce at
   0.25 to 0.36. `stitch_type`, `extra` and `other` essentially never
   reproduce: one reader's call of that class, alone, should not start a
   worker.
6. **`ruins` is more stable than `noticeable`** (7 of 10 places against 36%),
   on a small count.

**So, before a fault is worth a worker:** it should be placed by at least two
independent readers, same class, within 3 mm. On one reader's card that is
not knowable. That is why this was measured: two days of fixes were chosen
from single readings, and several of those faults turned out to be misreads.

## Recommendation (Kent's decision; the nightly procedure is unchanged)

**Two readers per design each night; act only on faults both place** (same
class within 3 mm, which `repeat` already computes when given the two verdict
folders), and read a grade move as real only when both readers agree on the
new grade.

- **Cost:** one reader is about 105,000 tokens (this run: 100,000 to 108,000
  each, 27 readers about 2.8 million). Two readers on nine designs is about
  1.9 million tokens a night, against about 950,000 now: **about 950,000
  more a night.**
- **What it buys:** on this run, the faults both of a pair place are the 38%
  of places found by two or more readers; most of the one-reader 62% drop out.
  Agreement on the grade between two readers is 7 of 9 to 9 of 9 designs.
- **The catch:** a real fault that only one reader of two happens to pin is
  dropped (by this run, a reproducible fault is missed by a given second
  reader more than half the time), so the card gets shorter and more
  trustworthy, not more complete. Three readers with "two of three" would
  keep more (64 of 167 places here) at about 2.8 million tokens a night.

The raw readings and the report json stay in the gitignored
`digitizer/eye_pairs_out/prime-repeat-2026-11-19/` with the kit (client
artwork); nothing from there is committed.
