- `L_mfab_hat` (Okay): The small 'AND WELDING LLC' line holds it back: its white backing is thin, ragged and partly reduced to loose strings along the top, the black letters have swallowed it, and the LL is muddled. The big M-FAB outline is clean, even satin and reads right, with only a few messy joins and a ragged mark inside the M. Many long needle-up moves criss-cross the piece and would need trimming.
- `L_precision_drone` (Bad): The emblem does not read as the art: the drone is a scatter of unrelated grey satin fragments on a black slab, and the blue sky and blue water of the right half are gone entirely (black and grey instead), so the thermal/normal split that is the point of the logo is lost.
