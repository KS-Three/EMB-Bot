# T-shaped slabs as their own column: `satin_slab_serifs` (2026-10-07)

- `fremont_TRN_off_above_on_below.png` — the three letters of HOTEL FREMONT
  at 80 mm that the flag moves, re-planned from the same stages 1–4 with
  `satin_slab_serifs` OFF (above) and ON (below): thin stitch lines, one
  colour per satin run, over the artwork (blue). Left to right: the T of
  HOTEL, the R and the N of FREMONT.
  - **T**: OFF the foot has no column — its halves ran into the foot's
    corners and the twig rule took them for a cap's I-beam, so the stem
    ends on the baseline and the foot's protrusions sew as nothing but the
    stem's last crosses (0.48 mm² of the bottom millimetre bare). ON the
    foot is a 2.5 mm column of seven crosses (0.02 mm² bare; +14 stitches,
    +1 hop). The bar and its two hanging slabs are byte-identical.
  - **R**: OFF the stem's column bends into the foot's right half and fans;
    ON the stem ends square and the foot is its own column.
  - **N**: OFF the left foot is a half-column fanned from the stem and the
    top-right serif is two half-columns meeting at the stem — the junction
    stack's 30° weld refused both pairs (144° and 148°). ON each is one
    column across the slab (foot bare 0.12 → 0.01 mm²).
- Every other letter is unchanged, the E's and F's middle arms included:
  their slabs protrude too little for the skeleton to grow a half either
  side (see scope-history 2026-10-07). Design: 13,746 → 13,780 stitches,
  34 trims both ways.
- The foot columns' crosses lean ~30° — the house angle's lean cap on a
  short crossbar, the same on the M's feet OFF — where the pro's feet sew
  square. That is the lean rule, not this flag.
