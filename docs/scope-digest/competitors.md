## Capability gaps vs Ember specifically

- **Fill-pattern library.** Ember: 13 enumerated from shipped bundles, 6 free / 7 paid (`ember-technical-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 4`); marketing says 24 (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 2`). EMB-Bot ships effectively one (tatami; contour off-by-default, blend photo-only). **Claimed needed:** yes — "the single biggest feature-count gap", pick 4–6 decorative fills as fast-follow (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.2`).
- **Decorative run/outline types.** Ember 7 (Single, Triple, Satin, E-Stitch, Double Rope, Triple Rope, Manual). EMB-Bot has no rope/E-stitch. **Claimed a gap**, no build recommendation (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 2`).
- **Gradient as a universal fill modifier** (Ramp/Plateau on any pattern) vs EMB-Bot's photo-tier-only blend stage. **Claimed needed and likely cheaper than new patterns** (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.3`).
- **Direct-to-machine transfer.** `ember-bridge`: Tauri v2 (Rust + React) desktop app terminating Brother's TLS 1.2 static-RSA "pedxml" protocol on `127.0.0.1:17831` (`emberdesign-competitive-research-2026-08-07.md § Pass 3`). Largest gap; docs ask for an explicit yes/no rather than drift (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.5`, `§ 5 Q2`).
- **Stitch player** (scrubber, speed multiplier, color-change markers, needle crosshair) + Realistic View toggle. **Claimed needed, "likely cheap to close", front-end only** (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.4`; detail in `emberdesign-competitive-research-2026-08-07.md § Pass 2`).
- **Export breadth.** Ember 9–10 formats client-side vs EMB-Bot's 4 in the browser path. **Claimed low-lift** because the Python side already routes through pyembroidery (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.6`).
- **Custom font upload** (TTF/OTF/TTC), **cloud autosave / version history / cross-device**, **15,809 threads × 78 brands**, **standalone free converter**, **Explore gallery + public profiles**. All logged as gaps; only the converter is called low priority, the gallery is deliberately parked (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 2`).
- **Multi-underlay as a first-class array.** Ember ships `underlays[]` with per-pass validation; EMB-Bot has single-string `fill_underlay`/`satin_underlay`. **Claimed worth copying "from day one"** (`ember-technical-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 4`, `§ 8`).
- **Not gaps — EMB-Bot ahead:** 72 vs 25 fonts; CIEDE2000 thread snapping (no vendor documents perceptual matching); per-region PCA fill angle vs Ember's entire angle logic `n = 90 * (t >= r)` (`ember-technical-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 5`); no ML anywhere in Ember's bundles (`§ 1`, `§ 5`).

## Techniques worth adopting

Licence posture: everything Ember-derived was read from minified production bundles — **concept-level clean-room only, no transcription of decompiled JS**. Ink/Stitch is **GPL-3.0: concepts only, never code or near-verbatim translation** (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 5.3`).

- **Fill patterns as data.** Each Ember pattern is a `stitchPattern` function returning `[{rowOffsetMm, rowPatternMm}, …]`; a tatami stagger is just `[{rowOffsetMm: t/4, rowPatternMm:[t]}, …]`. New patterns become table entries, not algorithms (`ember-technical-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 4`).
- **Versioned document + migration chain.** Ember is at v7+, observed v3→v4 and v6→v7, including a single `underlay` object migrated to `underlays[]` with backfilled `underlayContourCapInset: 2` (`ember-technical-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 3`).
- **Ember's shipped defaults as reference numbers:** underlay 90° off overlay (225 vs 135) inset 0.4 mm; satin `density 0.32`, auto-split above 7 mm with 4-cycle 0.3 mm stagger; gradient `{type:"ramp", start:0, end:1, endRowSpacingMm:1}`; per-underlay validation messages (`ember-technical-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 4`).
- **Color-block sequencer view** — collapse shapes into color blocks with thread name/brand/shape count and the stitch-index range each covers (`emberdesign-competitive-research-2026-08-07.md § Pass 2`); promoted because it maps to an already-found cross-color sequencing gap (`docs/scope/research-backlog.md`).
- **Expose the streamline fill EMB-Bot already has.** Ember ships `ess` evenly-spaced streamlines as a paid fill; `stage6_streamline.py` is a clean-room Jobard & Lefer implementation, currently photo-lane only (`emberdesign-competitive-research-2026-08-07.md § Pass 3`, `docs/scope/research-backlog.md`).
- **Goldman / SoftSight family — FREE, all expired ~2018-08-17** (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 5.1`), described to reimplementation depth in `§ 1`: DT-variance satin/fill test (`2σ < μ < ½·max` on skeletal-DT stats, a uniformity test not a width test); 16-candidate-angle fragment-count minimization; least-squares rail pairing with placeholders; singularity bridging by occluded-boundary reconstruction; the three-part serif absorption rule; mitre policy (extend one member through, shorten the others); interruption-magnitude sequencing objective; fixed vertices at shared borders.
- **Pulse US6390005B1 dynamic density inset — FREE (expired 2018-05-14).** Hold interstitch distance constant, let the inset float; advance by perpendicular distance to the previous stitch segment, solve `isd² = cubic(t)` (linear/quadratic degradation acceptable). Published calibration at 0.4 mm: 26.8 % @ 30°, 58.6 % @ 45°, 100 % @ 60° — so the industry's fixed 25–33 % is only right near 30° (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 1 Stage 6b`).
- **Wilcom UT-space curved fill — FREE (expired 2019-09-07)** and **WO2005061774A1 turning fill — never granted anywhere, free worldwide** (level-set cut lines, angle-bisector sampling at concave vertices, separation-class cliques) (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 5.1`).
- **Melco US9702070B2 — LIVE to ~2029.** Push-compensation physics (+10 % along the stitch axis, −5 % across; end cutbacks 0.4–0.7 mm by end width; suppress where an end intersects a neighbour) sits in the **specification**, not the granted claims — implement from physics, not text. The claimed underlay graph needs a design-around: build from EMB-Bot's own skeleton and duplicate a minimal T-join instead of every edge (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 5.2`, `§ 2 G5/G15`).
- **Brother US8200357B2 photo angle field — ACTIVE to ~2028.** Read for architecture only (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 5.2`).
- **Ink/Stitch methods (GPL-3.0, concepts only):** interior-biased travel weighting — boundary edges `3 × length`, interior edges `length / (distance_to_outline + 0.1)`, over gratings at fill-angle ±45° @ 2 mm and −90° @ 1.414 mm (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 3.1`, `research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 5.9`); Eulerian fill rows with a segment-biased edge chooser (`§ 3.2`); Zhao-Saalfeld tolerance-sleeve running stitch (`§ 3.3`); 3.0 mm jump-collapse to a plain stitch (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 5.9`); underlay inset as **negative pull compensation** through one shared rail-offset primitive (`§ 5.1`); progressive short-stitch inset levels (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 3.9`); penetration-crowding density map, red at 6 within 0.5 mm (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 6.1`).
- **stitch_generator — MIT, code actually reusable.** Adopt its abstraction whole: `Path = shape fn + width fn + direction fn`, which composes directly with a medial axis whose radius function *is* the width function (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 5.3`).
- **libembroidery — zlib.** Use as a *format-layout* third opinion (`src/formats/*.c`) and its `data/samples/` as free extra corpus (`embroidermodder-research-2026-08-13.md § 8`).
- **TurtleStitch (read-only reference; the changes are to EMB-Bot's own code):** emit a color change as its own zero-delta `0x00,0x00,0xC3` record instead of folding the flag onto pending movement; preserve the original stitch/jump flag across every sub-record of an oversized-move split and divide evenly rather than clamp-and-subtract; treat glyph curve-flattening point count as a clamped parameter (TurtleStitch clamps 5–60) instead of EMB-Bot's hardcoded 8 segments baked once and geometrically scaled (`turtlestitch-stitch-appearance-research-2026-08-14.md § 1`, `§ 2`, `§ 4`).
- **shape-tracer pipeline** — background-distance threshold → marching squares → Douglas-Peucker → Catmull-Rom-as-cubic-Bezier, with a `MIN_AREA` speckle filter verified against ~5500 dithered noise pixels; auto-trace-then-adjust is a good "starting stitch path" model (`shape-tracer-evaluation.md`). **Licence not stated anywhere in that doc — establish it before any port.**
- **Streamlines paper (CC BY-NC-ND; reference repo licence unverified, so clean-room):** density field as a conservation law, sources only where `div Z > 0`, global minimum-weight bipartite sink matching (greedy is provably wrong), MST-then-DFS routing for zero trims per colour, and joint thread-pair colour selection with a `10·max{C(s1,s2) − C(t1,t2), 0}` contrast-deficit term — a one-line addition to existing CIEDE2000 snapping (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 7`).
- **Business-model reference:** Ember charges for automation, not manual tooling — all manual tools free and unlimited, Pro ($9.99/mo) gates auto-digitize, click-to-stitch, custom fonts, gradient, extra fills/runs, storage. Useful tier-boundary reference when billing is untabled (`ember-technical-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 7`, `ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.7`).

## Explicitly rejected or out of scope

- **libembroidery as a dependency** — no capability gain (writes what EMB-Bot already writes), digitizing explicitly unbuilt upstream, 1.0-alpha mid-rewrite, bus factor 1, C FFI with `const int8_t*` filenames, no packaging; and the ecosystem already migrated off it to pyembroidery (`embroidermodder-research-2026-08-13.md § 8`). Also rejected: shelling out to `embroider` in the service path, and citing its in-memory "+y is up" comment as DST-axis evidence — that is a different layer (`§ 3.2`).
- **PEmbroider** — GPL-3 **plus Anti-Capitalist Software License v1.4**, which prohibits commercial software development. Commercially unusable; its hatching taxonomy is a catalogue of what to build, nothing else is takeable (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 5.3`).
- **Any Ink/Stitch source on disk beside EMB-Bot** — GPL-3.0 (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 5.3`).
- **Porting any Hatch spacing figure** — the "distance between two forward rows" wording may make Hatch numbers 2× EMB-Bot's units; deliberately not guessed (`hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 2.3`). Also a trap: the 2.0/4.0 mm figures on `Underlay_settings.htm` are image captions, not defaults (`§ 2.4`).
- **Changing `TRIM_AT_MM`, `MAX_STITCH_MM`, `SATIN_MAX_WIDTH_MM` or any density constant on Hatch's authority** — Hatch either confirms them or says nothing, and the corpus outranks it (`hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 8`).
- **Raising `SATIN_MAX_WIDTH_MM` to 10 mm** — Wilcom supports it only with a working per-stitch-length auto-split at 7 mm; ours gates on column width (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 12`).
- **Goldman's 45° satin-corner split rule** — rejected in favour of the corpus, which counted 1436 through-sewn corners against 18 splits (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 1 Stage 6b`).
- **Melco's "filter stitches below 5 points"** — it is a decimation of every *other* short stitch, a materially different algorithm; do not copy as a floor (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 4`, `§ 10`).
- **Scraped SEO density tables** — vendors' "SPI" labels are actually Melco points; never ingest (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 10`).
- **Hatch decorative tiers, PARKED against PRODUCT.md's non-goals:** motif stitching, stipple fills, cross-stitch fill, embossed fills, organic/hand-stitch and feathered edges (`hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 6`).
- **TurtleStitch — nothing to port** for travel routing (EMB-Bot's `travel_path` is a superset and adds a detour cap TurtleStitch lacks), tie/lock stitches, underlay, pull/push compensation, satin rails, or density heuristics; and there is no adaptive flattener there to port (`turtlestitch-stitch-appearance-research-2026-08-14.md § 3`, `§ 6`, `§ 7`, `§ Not relevant`).
- **Ember's own defects, logged to avoid not adopt:** non-scrolling fills gallery (hiding at least one paid fill), Properties popover overflowing the viewport, project name/thumbnail not syncing to the library, dead context menus, and a manual whose documented shortcuts no longer match the shipped toolbar (`emberdesign-competitive-research-2026-08-07.md § Pass 2`).
- **shape-tracer as-is** — same-colour touching shapes merge into one contour, and vertices carry no tangent handles, so corner edits spike; both need deliberate handling rather than a straight port (`shape-tracer-evaluation.md`).
- **Size-proportional `simplify_tol_mm`** — investigated and closed, no change: EMB-Bot already applies the tolerance after a real mm conversion, and Ember's own 0.32 mm floor is coarser than EMB-Bot's entire default (`docs/scope/research-backlog.md`).
- **Replacing the pipeline with a model** — no published end-to-end learned vector-art → machine-stitch system exists; classical CV plus measured constants is the field's state of the art (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 3.8`).

## Sequence claims

- **Fix the two correctness bugs before trusting any parity comparison.** DST axis transposition + the `0x43`/`0xC3` colour-change byte, and `FILL_ROW_MM = 0.40` vs ~0.20 — "they block trusting *any* other comparison in this document" (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.1`).
- **Do not change `FILL_ROW_MM` on analysis** — sew-out block 2 (0.40 vs 0.20 vs interleaved two-pass) is the decisive experiment and must come first (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 1`, `§ 12.1`).
- **`TRAVEL_ON_EDGE_ABOVE_MM = 0.9` is blocked on** settling the forward-rows unit question, or the constant is wrong by 2× (`hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 8.1` ← `§ 2.3`).
- **Hoist the distance transform to a shared early stage (G10) before** the DT-variance tier test (G1), per-branch tier assignment (G2), and least-squares rail pairing (G11); **G2 comes after G1** (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 2`).
- **Directional pull comp (G6) requires the fill angle at stage 5**, which is not available today — that is the real cost (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 2`).
- **Penetration-point knockout (G7) needs an A/B against the same-thread corridor before it lands**; **G11 needs the spray metric as its acceptance test**; **curved fill (G14) is blocked on deciding where guide curves come from** (medial axis or direction field) (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 2`).
- **Compute pull compensation first, then inset underlay from the compensated rail** — ordering is load-bearing, and Ink/Stitch appears to get it wrong (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 5.1`).
- **Satin ceiling work is sequenced after the width→density ramp** (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 12`).
- **Size-gate lettering underlay only after confirming** whether the lettering path gates on glyph height at all today; **widen `Fabric` to four object groups only when pull comp is next revisited** (`hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 8.2`, `§ 8.6`).
- **New preflight gates warn before they block** — every threshold needs a pass over the existing fixture set first (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 6.3`, `§ 12.5`).
- **Fill-pattern parity is incremental:** 4–6 patterns as fast-follow, not 24 at once, and use 13 as the conservative planning number pending a re-check against the live editor (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.2`, `§ 6`).
- **Standing priority rule, not a gate:** parity work is picked up only when nothing trust/quality-related is open and actionable, and an item earns priority only if it maps to an independently-flagged gap (`docs/scope/research-backlog.md`).

## Contradictions

- **Ember's fill-pattern count has three values across three docs:** 13 enumerated from the bundles (`ember-technical-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 4`), 24 from marketing (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 2`), and "24 of 30" Pro-gated (`emberdesign-competitive-research-2026-08-07.md § Pass 2`). Self-flagged unreconciled (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 6`).
- **Decorative fills are a PRODUCT.md non-goal** while `digitizer/` already ships contour and gradient-blend tiers and `ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.2` recommends adding 4–6 more. Flagged as open in both `hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 6` and `ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.8`; still undecided.
- **The manual-vector-tool gap row is stale.** `ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 2` says "No freehand draw / basic-shapes tool — explicit non-goal in `PRODUCT.md`"; PRODUCT.md now records manual draw mode as shipped and the basic shapes tool as launch item 4 ✅ Done.
- **"Launch blocker" framing conflicts with the launch bar.** `ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.1` calls the DST axis and fill density launch-blockers; PRODUCT.md's 2026-08-11 ruling is that engine quality is a parallel investment, *not* a launch gate, neither item appears on the launch checklist, and MASTER_SCOPE has the DST codec fix gated on Kent's call plus a sew-out.
- **The fill-spacing convention is simultaneously settled and unresolved.** `research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 1` declares Law 19 settled from four lineages plus two independent arithmetic checks; `hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 2.3` (a day later) records the same wording as unresolved and forbids porting any Hatch spacing figure. Consumers of one will contradict consumers of the other.
- **Three different satin-vs-fill discriminators are each recommended:** curvature/turning (`hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 1.1`), skeletal-DT variance (`masters-teardown-2026-08-01.md § 1 Stage 3`), and Melco's 70th-percentile stitch-width lookup (same section). The docs agree the current `2·area/perimeter` is wrong and disagree on the replacement.
- **Streamline fill is listed as an Ember-only capability** (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 2`) but EMB-Bot already ships the algorithm, scoped to the photo lane (`docs/scope/research-backlog.md`). The gap is exposure, not capability — the comparison row overstates it.
- **Napped-fabric density direction is contested:** trade press wants fleece density *increased* 10–15 % with underlay nearly doubled; EMB-Bot's presets *decrease* it (fleece ×0.90, terry ×0.85). Neither side is vendor-doc; belongs on a sew-out card (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 8`).
- **Pull-comp values exceed every published source** (terry 0.60, fleece 0.50 vs Wilcom's 0.40 ceiling) **and the unit is undetermined** — per-side vs total width gain changes whether EMB-Bot matches or is at half (`research-handoff-2026-08-07.md § 3.3`).
- **Knits underlay:** Hatch says flatly "on knits, edge run is best"; EMB-Bot gives knits the heavier `edge_lattice`. Called a divergence, not a defect (`hatch-manual-teardown-2026-08-08.md § 3.2`).
- **The format-library name differs across docs:** `ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 4.6` scopes export expansion on `pyembroidery`, while `docs/scope/research-backlog.md` has the MIT `pystitch` swap in progress — the low-lift claim needs restating against whichever library lands.
- **The community gallery is both a growth channel to revisit** (`ember-competitive-teardown-2026-08-09.md § 5 Q4`, citing 25k users / 40k designs) **and a declined non-goal** in PRODUCT.md on copyright/moderation grounds.
- **`shape-tracer-evaluation.md` carries no date, no source URL, and no licence statement**, and is indexed by neither MASTER_SCOPE.md nor `docs/scope/research-backlog.md` — unlike every other doc here, its portability posture cannot be assessed as written.
