# Scope digest — stage 0 classifier / photo & gradient lanes

## What the classifier gets wrong

- **10 of 15 real customer logos classify `gradient` at confidence 1.00 and go down the photo lane.** `gradient_smoothness` reads 0.205–6.135 on real art against `GRAD_VAR_GRADIENT_MIN = 0.0015`, whose own calibration comment records the fixture band it was set on ("every flat fixture <0.0006; every gradient ramp and the real drone_render >=0.005") — 137x–4090x over threshold. `tires_hat_3d` is a solid black script wordmark. `classifier-misroutes-real-logos-2026-08-15.md` §2.
- **The 5 that route `flat` do so only by reading exactly 0.000 on both signals** — Becker is a 2-colour PNG, 146x91 px, effectively no anti-aliasing. Ordinary AA edges are enough to misroute a flat logo. `classifier-misroutes-real-logos-2026-08-15.md` §2.
- **Class depends on export resolution — up to three classes for one image.** `becker_hat_large`: photo_scene @146px, photo_scene @400, flat @900, gradient @2000. `hotel_fremont_hat`: flat/gradient/gradient/gradient. Cause: `_gradient_smoothness` / `_unique_color_mass` computed on raw pixels with `GRAD_VAR_WIN = 5`, `CANNY_DILATE_PX = 3`. `…stage0-flat-gradient-recalibration-design.md` §1.
- **The photo gate is resolution-broken too**: Becker reads ucm 0.450 at 146 px against `UCM_PHOTO_MIN = 0.28`, hence `photo_scene`. Same spec §4.
- **Class also depends on the resampler.** `photo/enthusiast_logo.png` is `flat` native (1400x316) and `gradient` at 500 px under NEAREST/BILINEAR/BICUBIC/LANCZOS alike; at 1000 px NEAREST says flat and the other three say gradient. NEAREST interpolates nothing and posts the highest reading (23.1). Same spec §7.
- **`gradient_smoothness` is inverted for this population**: `bridge` (flat) 6.135 vs `drone` (gradient) 4.463; `mfab_hat`, a flat outlined script, is the corpus maximum. It measures edge/AA energy, so busy thin-stroke logos score high and smooth photographs low. Same spec §5b.
- **Misroute cost, measured**: `cfg.forced_class='flat'` on the ten = +4.85 mean, +3.2 corpus (42.5 → ~45.7); better on 8, worse on 1 (`hotel_fremont_hat` −4.3), unchanged on 1 (`tires_hat_3d` +0.1). `classifier-misroutes-real-logos-2026-08-15.md` §3.
- **Misrouted designs never reach the satin/fill ladder at all.** `bridge_lc`'s 83 regions all carry `tier=None`; sweeping `cfg.fill_angle_deg` over 0/15/30/45/60/75/90/None gives byte-identical output (10395 stitches, 47.9). Our fill-cell angles: std 84.6° vs pro 11.3°. Same doc §1.
- **`MFab` is tagged 0 enclosed regions because it classifies `CLASSIFIED_GRADIENT` + `BACKGROUND_ABSENT` and fragments into 64 regions** — "a two-colour line logo routed as a gradient photo… probably larger" than the enclosed-background issue. `enclosed-background-verdict-2026-08-15.md` §7.
- **`fur_ramp.png` classifies outside `gradient`** (8 flat circles) so it never reaches the blend tier — the classifier problem in the other direction. `blend-tier-never-fires-2026-08-15.md` §1. `logo_script_tires.png`, a clean two-colour script wordmark on white, classifies `photo_scene` outright (MASTER_SCOPE.md).
- **`alpha_softness` is computed into `signals` and never consulted by the decision tree** (≤0.019 on all 12 fixtures). `classifier-lens-2026-08-04.md`.
- **`GRAD_VAR_SUBJECT_MIN = 8.0` (margin 3.0) has never met a hard case** — its only two data points sit at 0.66 and 184.2, a 280x spread; placement is evidence-free. Same doc. MASTER_SCOPE adds: the `photo_subject` gate is bimodal, so textured subjects on smooth backdrops cannot reach it.
- Thinnest live margin on committed fixtures: `bg_uncertain.png` (a solid navy rectangle) sits 0.0005 — 32% headroom — under the effective gradient flip point, and is the only fixture below confidence 1.00 (0.77). Same doc.

## Rejected approaches

Stage 0 recalibration spec (`…stage0-flat-gradient-recalibration-design.md`), all measured not reasoned:

1. **Recalibrate `GRAD_VAR_GRADIENT_MIN` against real art** — rejected §1/§4: no threshold can be correct while one image yields three classes by resolution.
2. **5a — resample every input to a fixed working resolution (1000 px)**: neither separates (mfab flat 36.25 > drone gradient 5.61 > bridge flat 3.26 > becker flat 0.09) nor stabilises (becker still flips between 400 and 900 px source); LANCZOS ringing is itself gradient variance.
3. **5b — keep `gradient_smoothness`, re-site its threshold**: does not separate at any scaling including native; inverted relative to intent.
4. **5c — size the windows to the image (0.5% window / 0.3% dilation) instead of the image to the windows**: much worse — invariance spread over a 146–2000px sweep goes 28.9x → 19422.4x (hotel), 7.3x → 2437.5x (tires), 7.9x → 522.7x (gaulke), 7.2x → 54.1x (mfab). "The result that matters most in this spec."
5. **Supplementing the label set from synthetic fixtures** (`gradient_ramp_linear/radial`, `summit_badge`, `drone_render`) — rejected §6 as reintroducing the exact fixture bias that caused the miscalibration. **Bilateral denoising** to widen the 17-vs-19 gap — rejected, pulls `drone` down as much as `bridge`.
6. **Shipping invariance separately from the new signal** — an earlier §7 draft claimed it; §5c disproves it. Not separable.

Elsewhere:

- **Cap the RAG hierarchical merge on region-internal Lab spread** (`photo-quality-root-cause-2026-08-11.md` #6.2) — built and reverted. A tightening factor cannot bound anything (only defers a merge; `drone_render` worst internal RMS 35.88 off → 62.97 at cap 16.0; region count non-monotonic). The ceiling is SEEDS granularity, not the merge: `drone_render`'s worst raw superpixel is 70.00 RMS and its worst final region is 70.00 at every cap. `SEEDS_TARGET_FG_SUPERPIXELS` 1200→3600 moves 35.88→35.61. Below cap ~14 the 20–80 accept band blows out (21 → 170 regions), undoing PR #45. Cap 16.0 bought nothing on its two targets and cost `repro_gradient_white_icon` 12 points.
- **Lowering `RAMP_R2_MIN`** (`blend-tier-never-fires-2026-08-15.md` §2) — refused: a fit explaining 9% of variance is not a ramp. **Region-slicing hypothesis** (quantisation destroys the ramp, so bigger regions fit better) — rejected: `r(area, r2) = −0.226` on `drone_render`, largest region near-worst at 0.014.
- **`blend_tonal_bands`** (lightness banding inside the fill tier, `0923b91`) — built, decomposed geometry correctly, changed nothing visible because shades share one thread; removed in `e460ceb`. Same doc §4.
- **Whole-design ramp detection on L\* alone** — measured r2 = 0.003 on the repro; the purple→pink→orange sweep is hue rotation, `b*` carries it at 0.45. Replaced by `detect_design_ramp_angle` fitting L, a, b independently, gated at `DESIGN_RAMP_R2_MIN = 0.4`. `…gradient-tier-fragmentation…md` Defect 1 update.
- **Direction 1 (route `gradient` through `stage2_photo_segment` SLIC+RAG)** — recommended against in favour of 2+3, as a fragment-count proxy rather than the angle-consistency defect. Same doc.
- **Alpha-as-ground-truth for enclosed background** — would not have helped: `repro_gradient_white_icon.png` is a 3-channel PNG with no alpha. Same doc, Defect 2 update.
- **Garment-driven stroke-width floor** — rejected: at *skeleton* pixels Becker is the thickest design (p05 1.44 mm) while `precision_drone` reads 0.30; the rule fires on the designs the pro left alone and stays silent on the one he filled. Sign backwards. `enclosed-background-verdict-2026-08-15.md` §1/§7. **Flipping the enclosed-background default** — rejected: +8.0/design is `coverage` IoU rewarding overlap while `density` fell 0.66→0.48 and `sttype` to 0.00, and every enclosed region inherits `thread_index 155`, the enclosing thread.
- **DT-first classifier as the note specifies** (`dt-classifier-spike-2026-08-02.md`): the max-radius endpoint trim (every arm monotonically worse, 921.8 ms → 4285.6 ms, and on an 18 mm blob with a 2 mm tail it deletes the blob's skeleton and flips fill→satin); the `mu > max/2` term (identical verdicts on all 49 shapes); `2*max <= cap` as the thinness test (rejects 4 of 8 serif glyphs — `t` reads `dt_max` 5.67 mm on 3.64 mm strokes). Arm `MIN2` scored 0/21 and is disqualified by the artwork. The professional corpus is rejected as referee for a per-shape rule (population moved 128→216→301 across instrument fixes and reordered the arms each time).
- **Flipping `photo_segment_sam2` to True** — three ties and ~40 s CPU/job. **Option B, server-side segmentation** — rejected on the local-first/no-account promise. **Option A as installer bundle** — forces ~1 GB on everyone; D (opt-in download) chosen, sequenced as C. `sam2-segmentation-live-acceptance-2026-08-10.md` §8, `sam2-ship-path-brief-2026-08-11.md`. **Raising `points_per_side` 16→32→48** — one extra mask for triple wall-clock, then the 180 s subprocess timeout fired.
- **rembg in the shared venv** — `numba 0.66.0` refuses numpy ≥2.5 and the repo pins 2.5.1; downgrading numpy was rejected against the goldens' exact pins. Option 2 (isolated venv, numpy 2.4.6) shipped. `opencv-contrib-python` (GUI) rejected for the headless twin at the same `5.0.0.93` pin. `photo-prep-deps-probe-2026-08-04.md`.
- Standing exclusions (`photo-digitizing-plan-2026-07-31.md` §5): one-click photorealism, neural stitch generation, TSP-art tour rendering, GPL code (Ink/Stitch, StippleGen), bria-rmbg (CC BY-NC), dlib 68-point, mediapipe, GPU, alpha matting, full-frame uniform-density photo fill.

## Sequence claims

- **Downstream photo/gradient work is wasted while the routing is wrong.** `classifier-misroutes-real-logos-2026-08-15.md` §7: "on real customer artwork the problem is upstream of that — those designs should mostly not be in the gradient lane at all." The `bridge_lc` fill-angle investigation was the concrete casualty: a fill-angle bug that does not exist because the design never reaches `stage6_fill`'s angle path. `blend-tier-never-fires-2026-08-15.md` §4 excludes the 15 real logos from the `split_tonal_regions` corpus run for the same reason, and Kent's 2026-08-16 tabling is explicitly "in favour of getting non-gradient artwork right first."
- **The instrument must be fixed before the fix is measurable.** `photo-quality-root-cause-2026-08-11.md` #6.2: making `preflight._artwork_colors_by_thread` per-region "is a prerequisite for #6.2 being measurable, not a side quest" — the pooled per-thread median cannot see a segmentation fix at all. Proven by #6.3: for one day the repo held a real fix and a broken instrument and recorded the instrument's verdict; after `619e9ad` made `THREAD_MATCH_POOR` per-region, `repro_gradient_white_icon` reads F/0 → B/76.
- **Invariance and the replacement signal cannot be sequenced separately**, and neither can be built before the artwork arrives: "Nothing but the failing test in §7 is buildable." The scale-invariance regression test (`digitizer/tests/test_classifier_scale_invariance.py`, `strict=True` xfails) is the only shippable item, and its turning green *is* the acceptance criterion. `…stage0-flat-gradient-recalibration-design.md` §2/§5c/§7.
- **Both gates must become invariant together** — fixing only flat/gradient leaves `unique_color_mass` resolution-dependent. Same spec §4.
- **Golden churn from a recalibration is only judgeable in CI**, never on Windows: CI is `ubuntu-latest` and the three local failures are platform divergence against goldens captured there. Same spec §8; `enclosed-background-verdict-2026-08-15.md` §5.3.
- **SAM2's three gates before any build**: re-measure `points_per_side` on the real photo; confirm or refute the owl stage-1 hypothesis — "if the white-body-floods-as-background failure is `stage1_prep`, SAM2 ships behind a bug it cannot fix, and fixing stage 1 raises SLIC+RAG's floor too"; commit 2–3 real photo fixtures. Plus: the silent fallback must surface in the UI before SAM2 is advertised. `sam2-ship-path-brief-2026-08-11.md`.
- **Read the root-cause doc before assuming segmentation is the bottleneck**: PR #45 (SLIC+RAG replacing k-means for `gradient`) already shipped without moving any grade. `photo-quality-root-cause-2026-08-11.md` (context). Its own leverage order: (1) `max_colors` fix, (2) merge threshold — check for rebase conflict with the SAM2 branch's `kept_masks_to_quant` extraction in the same file, (3) post-vectorization colour check, which needed its own design pass.
- **Build order in `photo-digitizing-plan-2026-07-31.md` §4**: the classifier lands first specifically to protect the flat lane byte-identically; the contrib swap is gated on golden byte-verify (kmeans is exact-pinned); tier 10 (streamlines) does not ship publicly before the IP consult; "nothing in classes (c)/(d) is marketed before" the step-10 sew-out gate; steps 5+ should not be resumed before reading the gradient-fragmentation doc.

- **`becker-pro-parity-2026-08-15.md`**: 129 runs vs the pro's 15 is documented but not diagnosed — "that is the next piece of work."
- **Recalibrate on real art and validate on fixtures, not the reverse** — the calibration/validation inversion is named as the cause of the original miscalibration. `classifier-misroutes-real-logos-2026-08-15.md` §5.
