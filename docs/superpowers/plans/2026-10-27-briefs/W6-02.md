# W6-02: A timed-out digitize keeps the service busy — the customer's retry waits behind dead work

Model: claude-opus-5-5  Branch suggestion: claude/job-timeout-reclaim-w6
Files you own: digitizer/digitizer_service/jobs.py, digitizer/digitizer_service/guards.py if needed, digitizer/tests/test_service*.py (new tests), the narrowest cancel hook in digitizer_core you need, one memory note.
Gate: none (service plumbing, no stitch changes). Output for a job that finishes must stay byte-identical.

GOAL. jobs.py runs one worker thread (ThreadPoolExecutor). A job past JOB_TIMEOUT_S (600 s) is reported failed lazily on read, but "Python cannot stop a thread, so the work itself runs on and its result is thrown away" (jobs.py:44-47). Measured in PR #1149 (2026-10-27): after small_text_logo's 250 mm job was reported failed at 600 s, the NEXT job queued behind the still-running work for ~220 s before it started. The customer sees "took too long ... Try a smaller or simpler image", tries a smaller one, and waits minutes for it. MAX_INFLIGHT (4) also counts that dead work, so a few timeouts can 503 every submit.

PREMISE FIRST. Reproduce with a test, not a 10-minute digitize: a registry with a tiny timeout and a fake job that sleeps/loops, then a second job; measure when the second starts. Confirm on the real service once (one CPU slot take): a long job (small_text_logo at 250 mm jacket_back, asset from #1138/#1149) then vector_logo at 100 mm; record the second job's queue wait. If the overrun is already bounded to seconds, report and stop.

BUILD. Make a timed-out job give the worker back within seconds. Pick with evidence and say why in the PR:
- (a) Cooperative cancel: a per-job threading.Event passed down, checked at stage boundaries and inside the per-cluster/per-letter loops; raises a Cancelled the registry treats as the timeout. Measure the worst gap between checkpoints on the slow design — it is the overrun bound.
- (b) Run each digitize in a child process the registry can kill. Costs spawn time per job and pickling of config/results; check what the cache and the rembg/sam2 subprocess calls need.
Also: the timeout is checked "lazily on read" — make sure a job nobody polls still releases the worker.

DONE-TEST.
- Unit tests: second job starts within N s of the first's timeout (N = your measured checkpoint bound); a cancelled job's partial result never enters the cache; MAX_INFLIGHT no longer counts reclaimed work; a job that finishes normally returns byte-identical output with the hook in place.
- One real measurement before/after (the long-then-short pair above).
- Service tests green: cd digitizer && .venv/Scripts/python -m pytest -q -n 2 tests/test_service.py (+ your new file).
- PR ready-for-review, auto-merge armed while mergeable_state is blocked.

DO NOT TOUCH: the stitch output of any stage. W6-01 (speed, same design) works in digitizer_core hot spots — if your cancel checkpoints land in a function it is rewriting, message the foreman before editing it.
