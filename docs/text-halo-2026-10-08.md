# Failure C, halo strands in text: re-measured on today's engine (2026-10-08)

**Status: measured, nothing built.** Kent picked C (the text-scoped halo
dissolve) for the `claude/lettering-l2` lane on 2026-10-08. The first step
was to re-measure the problem, and the problem as billed is no longer
there.

## What was billed

The outline-cut write-up (2026-10-05, `29d85ea6`) gave C as "23–30% of the
stitches in Gaulke's text are not black". That doc's own caveat says its
inputs came from `cf9f89f1`, 135 commits behind `main` at the time. Two
reports carried the number forward: `lettering-architecture-rd-2026-10-07`
and its memory note.

## What it is now

The new `digitizer/tools/text_halo.py` uses the L1 hand labels
(`testdata/lettering_truth.json`) as the text, grown by 0.3 mm. It counts
every needle point inside that area by the shape it belongs to: the letters,
the ground (a region bigger than half the text's hull), or other.

| fixture | letters | other share of the text's stitches | what "other" is |
|---|---|---|---|
| gaulke (corpus, 14 px/mm) | 35 | **0.0%** | — |
| gaulke `art/` copy (607 px) | 35 (L1 words) | 4.4% | ~100 stitches of 0.11–0.15 mm slivers, threads 17 and 2: **real halo** |
| drone | 24 | 6.0% | 111 stitches of 0.07–0.10 mm slivers in thread 209 (**real halo**, 3.3%); the rest are O-counter pieces in the letters' own thread |

| becker | 11 | 30.8% | **not halo**: the band the BECKER letters are holes in (2,160 stitches), plus the counters |
| enthusiast, tires | 24, 4 | 0.0% | — |
| screenshot | 35 | 0.9% | one ground piece |

*(measured 2026-10-08: `python -m tools.text_halo`, then
`python -m tools.text_halo testdata/art/logo_golke_roofing.png:80`, on
`claude/lettering-l1-tagger` `0a6eab28` = `main` `e86b7d7` + #675)*

**Reading it.** The halo the plan describes now runs from 0 to about 4% of
a word's stitches, worst on the low-resolution gaulke copy and on drone.
It was 23–30% on the old engine. Several flips since then plausibly took
it:

- `robust_region_colour` and the colour bundle (09-10)
- `keep_thin_strokes` (09-13)
- `subpixel_edges` (09-09)

None of them was bisected here; that attribution is a guess.

**What is left:** drone's and the gaulke copy's sub-0.15 mm slivers in a
third thread. A text-scoped dissolve would remove them, but the price of
sewing them is a few dozen stitches and possibly one thread change. That is
Kent's call to make.
